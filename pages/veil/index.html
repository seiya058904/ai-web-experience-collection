VEIL

The space
between threads.

VEIL is an original digital installation about the quiet architecture of cloth. A continuous surface is woven, suspended, folded and opened into light.

The forms are made in real time. Move your pointer to stir the air; use the material controls to change what you see. Each composition can be explored in either direction.

An artistic material study, rather than a physical textile simulation. Original generated silk studies accompany procedural geometry. Research references, asset provenance and full license notices are included in the source package.
