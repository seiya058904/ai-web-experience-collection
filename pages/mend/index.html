About this story.

MEND is a small, original fiction about care: helping something old take part in life again, without taking away the things that make it familiar.

Pip, Lan, He and the two repair options are invented. The moving model is an illustration, not a real repair procedure or evidence that a physical toy is safe to use.

Use the chapter links at any time. Nothing needs to finish playing before you move on. Motion can be reduced; still views preserve the whole story and its controls.

The artwork is AI-generated and the interactive object is purpose-built. No account, tracking or external service is used while you visit. Your repair choice can stay in this browser.

Ideas that informed the care

We borrowed the principles of understanding wear, intervening with restraint, distinguishing new materials and recording changes.
