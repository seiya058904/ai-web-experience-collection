This is an authored digital exhibit. The study number belongs to this artwork. The images, sections and model do not represent an accessioned museum object, a measured fossil, or a real CT acquisition. No collecting locality, geological age or scanner resolution is assigned.

Its scientific thread is real: sediment can cover remains; several pathways preserve their form; preparation and imaging can make evidence available for continued study. The missing parts remain missing.
