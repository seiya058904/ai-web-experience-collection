About this experience

RESONANCE is an artistic study of acoustic motion. Visual time and displacement are adjusted so you can see the relationships. The plate patterns are inspired by nodal modes; they are not a calibration of a specific material.

All sound is synthesized in your browser. Capture holds the current tone for three seconds, without a microphone. The frequency control sets the audible fundamental; the visual motion is slowed.
