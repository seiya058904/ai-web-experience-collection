Begin the binding

Before the book,
a sheet.

Flat, but never without depth.
Light finds the fibres. A fold finds the grain.

Order begins
with a fold.

A crease gives the sheet an axis.
A gathering gives it depth.

Many parts.
One intention.

Folded sections find their order.
The space between them disappears.

A line becomes
structure.

Connection gives the pages a spine.

The quiet
backbone.

A thousand edges.
One continuous body.

An ordered
kind of chance.

The pattern becomes part of the binding.

A body,
held together.

The structure receives its enclosure.

Pressure leaves
an impression.

Type becomes part of the material.
A trace of gold catches the light.

A book.
At last.

Paper. Thread. Pressure.
Everything held. Nothing extra.

A DIGITAL CRAFT STUDY

Colophon.

Assembly through
order and pressure.

CODEX is an original digital study of a sewn, cloth-cased book. Its ten chapters bring together the quiet transformations of paper, thread, cloth and type.

Type
Cormorant Garamond
Manrope
Materials
Ivory paper · linen thread
Black bookcloth · marbled endpaper

An artistic interpretation of binding craft. The sequence brings related processes into one continuous view; it is not a manufacturing manual. The imagery is original and includes AI-assisted material studies.
