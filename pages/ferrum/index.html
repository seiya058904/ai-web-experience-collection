FERRUM

Steel remembers
the fire.

FERRUM is an interactive study of matter becoming form. Follow one piece of steel through heat, force, water and light.

Its memory is written in its surface: a ridge, a scar, a change in reflection. This is an original digital sculpture and a poetic interpretation of craft.

Scroll at your own pace. Move backward to revisit a moment. Stay as long as you like.

Your experience

Sound starts only when you switch it on. Still images preserve every chapter.
