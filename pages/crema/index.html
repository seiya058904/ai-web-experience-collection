About this study +

Nine bar is the classical reference for this work. Pre-infusion and pressure profiles vary.

The coffee bed is a porous material. Crema contains CO₂-rich bubbles within a liquid carrying extracted compounds, microscopic oils and fine particles.

Flow fields and microstructures are authored interpretations informed by coffee research.
