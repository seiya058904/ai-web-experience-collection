    URUSHI

    Layers of lacquer.

    An ordinary wooden form, made extraordinary by what happens to its surface.

    This is an original digital material study. A single vessel passes through ground, thin lacquer coats, humid curing, charcoal abrasion, repeated layers, polishing and maki-e. The reflection is the thread that connects them.

    The material is real. The object is imagined.

    Urushi is derived from tree sap. Its hardening involves moisture-dependent, enzyme-mediated oxidation and polymerization. The process is more than evaporation. Here, its time and scale are deliberately compressed.

    The mirror finish is inspired by roiro. The later conceal-and-reveal sequence draws on togidashi maki-e: a powdered design is covered with lacquer and then polished back into view. This journey brings together selected practices; it is not a universal workshop recipe.
