A small
fluid machine.

NIB follows stored ink through a feed, a slit and an attached meniscus, into paper and finally into a line. Paper draws. The feed supplies and buffers. Replacement air allows the exchange to continue.

This is an authored interpretation of a conventional fountain-pen system. Magnification, geometry and timing serve the experience. The final material is an aqueous dye ink on a lightly textured writing paper.
