FIELD NOTES

Light, with
some context.

An independent, research-inspired exhibition. “Language” is an invitation to look more closely—not a claim that these signals have been translated.

01

A colony, not a single jelly

Erenna siphonophores are connected colonies of specialized zooids. Research describes terminal luminous structures and red fluorescent material in mature lures. The small warm point in this exhibition is an artistic interpretation.

MBARI · Angler siphonophore ↗Haddock et al. · Science, 2005 ↗
02

Patterns, without a translation

Pattern A is inspired by bilaterally pale and dark pigmentation. Pattern B is inspired by countershading. Researchers propose that subcutaneous light may make such patterns visible in darkness; the signaling mechanism was not filmed in situ in the 2020 study. No meaning is assigned here.

Burford & Robison · PNAS, 2020 ↗MBARI · Humboldt squid research ↗
03

A rainbow needs a light to work with

Comb rows can produce rainbow effects from external light. This comparison isolates that effect; it does not say that comb jellies cannot also produce their own light.

Monterey Bay Aquarium · Comb jelly ↗MBARI · Bioluminescence ↗

About this observation window

Original AI-assisted artwork and calculated 2.5D lighting, pigment masks and paths. These are separate observation scenes, not a real dive or a claim that the animals share a habitat. A weak display fill preserves structure in the dark. Control values are relative settings, not calibrated light measurements.

The view is intentionally limited; it is not an all-angle 3D model. No institutional affiliation is implied. There is no audio, tracking or external media.

The full light, encounter and pattern controls are available in either view.
