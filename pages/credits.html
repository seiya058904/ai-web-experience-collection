AI Web Experience Collection is an independent showcase of five complete web experiences, built with AI-assisted design and engineering. Each work keeps its own visual language, interaction and pace.

Beyond the Limit

A Chinese introduction to Formula 1 engineering. The unbranded racing imagery is AI-generated concept illustration. Educational models are illustrative, not telemetry or race predictions. This is not an official Formula 1, FIA or team website.

Barlow Condensed and Noto Sans SC are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Circuit geometry by Tomislav Bacinger, f1-circuits, is MIT licensed.

Font, circuit and Lenis notices · Educational sources

VERDANT

An imagined spring landscape. Pavilion, fern, water and meadow plates are original AI-generated illustrations supplied with the complete VERDANT project, not photographs of real locations.

ORBITAL

An illustrative mission from Earth to deep space. The spacecraft, rendered components and cinematic plates are original illustrations supplied with ORBITAL. Simulated flight values are educational, not real mission telemetry. Independent of NASA and SpaceX.

Earth texture: NASA / Goddard Blue Marble. Moon texture: NASA / Goddard lunar visualization. Cloud texture: Reto Stöckli / NASA Goddard. Further scientific sources appear in ORBITAL's Sources & credits dialog.

GLAZE — Color Fired Into Form

Seven studies of ceramic form, glaze, color and light. Supplied original AI-generated ceramic illustrations depict imaginary vessels, not museum artifacts or workshop records. The original application retains its MIT license; Archivo uses the SIL Open Font License.

Project license · Archivo license

KAGE / VOID

Eight movements in light, shadow and space, composed from original procedural geometry. No stock photography, external models or prerecorded videos. Original code uses MIT; Barlow Condensed, DM Sans and Noto Sans JP use SIL OFL. Its gallery plate is captured from the actual FRAME scene.
