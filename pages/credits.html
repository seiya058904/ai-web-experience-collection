Eleven worlds.
Made to explore.

AI Web Experience Collection is an independent showcase of eleven complete web experiences, built with AI-assisted design and engineering. Each work keeps its own visual language, interaction and pace.

Beyond the Limit

A Chinese introduction to Formula 1 engineering. The unbranded racing imagery is AI-generated concept illustration. Educational models are illustrative, not telemetry or race predictions. This is not an official Formula 1, FIA or team website.

Barlow Condensed and Noto Sans SC are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Circuit geometry by Tomislav Bacinger, f1-circuits, is MIT licensed.

Font, circuit and Lenis notices · Educational sources

VERDANT

An imagined spring landscape. Pavilion, fern, water and meadow plates are original AI-generated illustrations supplied with the complete VERDANT project, not photographs of real locations.

Instrument Serif © 2022 The Instrument Serif Project Authors; DM Sans © 2014 The DM Sans Project Authors. Both use the SIL Open Font License.

Instrument Serif license · DM Sans license

ORBITAL

An illustrative mission from Earth to deep space. The spacecraft, rendered components and cinematic plates are original illustrations supplied with ORBITAL. Simulated flight values are educational, not real mission telemetry. Independent of NASA and SpaceX.

Earth texture: NASA / Goddard Blue Marble. Moon texture: NASA / Goddard lunar visualization. Cloud texture: Reto Stöckli / NASA Goddard. Further scientific sources appear in ORBITAL's Sources & credits dialog.

GLAZE — Color Fired Into Form

Seven studies of ceramic form, glaze, color and light. Supplied original AI-generated ceramic illustrations depict imaginary vessels, not museum artifacts or workshop records. The original application retains its MIT license; Archivo uses the SIL Open Font License.

Project license · Archivo license

KAGE / VOID

Eight movements in light, shadow and space, composed from original procedural geometry. No stock photography, external models or prerecorded videos. Original code uses MIT; Barlow Condensed, DM Sans and Noto Sans JP use SIL OFL. Its gallery plate is captured from the actual FRAME scene.

Project license · Library and font notices

CHRONOS — The Architecture of Time

An original mechanical watch explored through six cinematic perspectives. Photographic art is AI-generated; the procedural movement is an interpretive kinematic study, not a manufacturable calibre or an accuracy simulation. Sound is synthesized locally after activation. The original application and documentation retain MIT; Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono use SIL OFL.

Project license · Library and font notices

INTERVAL — Architecture Between Light & Space

A continuous passage through six original architectural spaces. Imagery is AI-generated concept visualization of a fictional coastal building, with no reference photography redistributed. Bodoni Moda and Manrope are locally hosted under SIL OFL; original application code and text retain MIT.

Project license · Library and font notices

FORM — Objects in Space

An interactive study of three original furniture objects: a curved plywood chair, a metal table lamp and a stone side table. Geometry, finishes and drafting details are procedural. Measurements are conceptual approximations, not manufacturing drawings or engineering specifications. DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono use SIL Open Font License; original code retains MIT.

Project license · Attribution and third-party notices

FUSION — Building a Star

An original procedural tokamak journey informed by ITER, the U.S. Department of Energy and PPPL. Temperatures, magnetic fields and feedback readings are illustrative; this is not a plasma simulation, experimental data set, or claim of net power. Manrope and IBM Plex Mono use SIL Open Font License; original code retains MIT.

Project license · Attribution and third-party notices · Scientific sources and limits

GLASSHOUSE — Light Through Matter

Six continuous spatial studies of glass and light, composed from original procedural geometry, real-time optical layers and a dedicated AI-generated light-projection texture. Refraction, caustics and reflections are authored visual approximations, not a physical optical simulator. Original code and projection texture retain MIT; Manrope uses SIL OFL.

Project license · Attribution and third-party notices · Manrope license

VEIL — Fabric in Motion

An original digital installation of thread, fabric, light and soft structure. Real-time geometry accompanies three supplied AI-generated silk studies. It is an artistic material study, not a physical textile simulation. Original code retains MIT; Cormorant Garamond and Manrope use SIL OFL. Its still edition retains readable studies and navigation.

Project license · Attribution and third-party notices · Cormorant Garamond license · Manrope license
