About the machine

An original, unbranded, high-bypass twin-spool turbofan. The inner LP shaft connects the fan, booster and rear turbine; the outer HP shaft connects the core compressor and forward turbine.

Geometry and airflow are authored illustrations. Motion is slowed to reveal the mechanism. The 9:1 flow split and field displays are illustrative, not measured telemetry.

Engineering background: NASA Glenn Research Center. Complete sources, attribution and licences are included in the project.
