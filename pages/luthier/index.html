The making
of a silence.

The object

An original digital interpretation of an unnamed violin. The photographic imagery was generated for this work; the structural study is an original parametric model.

It is not a photograph, scan or measured reconstruction of a historical instrument.

The movement

One string becomes grain, structure, tension and a field of sound. Time and displacement are deliberately enlarged so the transition can be seen.

The contours are artistic interpretations, not measured acoustic modes. Audio is original synthesized sound, enabled only by you.
