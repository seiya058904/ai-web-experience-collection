CHRONOS

A study
of time.

CHRONOS is an independent digital artwork. An original watch, imagined in metal, light and motion.

The interactive mechanism is a cinematic kinematic study. Gear ratios, opposing rotations and the rhythm of a 4 Hz balance inform its movement. The geometry is interpretive; it is not a manufacturable calibre or an accuracy simulation. The live mechanism is presented at ⅛ speed, with ¹⁄₃₂ speed available in Oscillation.

Made for this experience

Original procedural geometry and materials. Original AI-generated photographic artwork. Locally synthesized mechanical sound. Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono typography.

Built with

Three.js · GSAP / ScrollTrigger · Lenis · TypeScript / Vite.

01 — Time

The journey begins with a complete skeleton watch. Light catches the steel case, while fine hands span a landscape of bridges, wheels and jewel bearings. Scrolling moves the camera through the dial into the mechanism.

02 — Energy

Inside the open barrel, a coiled mainspring stores mechanical energy. As the spring unwinds, the barrel passes that energy to the wheel train.

03 — Transmission

Each wheel drives the next pinion. Adjacent externally meshing gears turn in opposite directions. Different tooth counts change their speeds. The assembly opens along its axles so you can see the separate layers and their relationships.

04 — Oscillation

The escape wheel is alternately locked and released by a pallet fork. Small impulses sustain the balance wheel, while the hairspring returns it toward the centre. The study depicts a nominal 4 Hz balance: four full oscillations, or eight beats, each second. Its physical motion is slowed for inspection.

05 — Craft

A polished bevel, a fine brushed surface, a blued screw and a jewel bearing fill the view. A tiny piece of engineering becomes a landscape.

06 — Chronos

The view pulls back. The individual structures return to one complete watch. Every part. One purpose. Time, made visible.
