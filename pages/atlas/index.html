Read the field notes

One place. Every scale.

The journey observes Fujiyoshida, Mount Fuji and the Fuji Five Lakes in Japan. It starts at 35.4875 degrees north, 138.8079 degrees east. A coordinate becomes a grid. Actual elevation contours rise together into three-dimensional terrain. Mapped waterways reveal lakes and river valleys. A real Landsat mosaic is registered to that terrain. As the camera approaches Fujiyoshida, aerial orthophotography reveals roads and building footprints. Those same footprints lift to schematic eight-metre buildings. A designed route follows actual mapped streets. An artistic daylight-to-night study changes the appearance of the same city. The camera pulls away continuously, leaving the original coordinate as one point on a cartographic Earth.

Scroll, use arrow keys or Page Down, or choose a chapter from the Index. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Geographic data is provided by the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan and Natural Earth.

THE INTERPRETATION
Building footprints are real; their uniform 8 m heights and window patterns are schematic. The route follows actual road geometry and is an exhibition path, not navigation advice. Sunlight and night lighting are artistic studies. No generated images are used as geographic evidence.
