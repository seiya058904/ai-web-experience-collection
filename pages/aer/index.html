Ground load
The simple idea behind it

Support is the weight of the outside air displaced by the fixed envelope volume. Total weight includes the inside air, envelope, basket, equipment and load. Increasing relative warmth reduces only the inside-air weight. Adding load increases only total weight.

This experiment stays on the ground. It does not change the journey, and nothing is dropped from the basket.

 A note from the ground

An imagined morning.
A real idea.

AER explores buoyancy and the freedom of moving with different layers of air. The landscape, people, wind, routes and timing are authored fiction. It is not weather, navigation, a flight record or flying instruction.

The ground experiment uses a fixed volume and normalized relative quantities. Outside-air support is compared with the combined weight of inside air, envelope, basket, equipment and load. The routes integrate a simple continuous wind field as height changes.

The cinematic images were made with AI. The live flight study, diagrams, controls and keepsake are rendered in code. Photography and the illustrated study are deliberately framed as different views.
