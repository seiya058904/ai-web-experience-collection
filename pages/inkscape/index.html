INKSCAPE is an interactive material study. Water becomes a boundary. Ink becomes a gesture. Paper becomes a space.

The textures are original generated studies. The spreading, bristles, fibers, folds and impressions are brought to life in your browser. Their behavior is an artistic interpretation of matter.

Move with your scroll, or use the movement index. In Trace, change the brush’s wetness. In Fiber, look closer. In Mark, leave something of yourself.

Typeset in Cormorant Garamond, Manrope and IBM Plex Mono. A quiet work for the open web.
