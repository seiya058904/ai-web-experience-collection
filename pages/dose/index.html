Preparing the material study.

One illustrative route

We follow a hypothetical, uncoated, immediate-release tablet through direct compression: prepared ingredients, blending, die filling, compaction, structure and contact with liquid. Granulation and film coating are not part of this chosen branch.

The particle forms, proportions, colors, motion and timing are conceptual. No real formulation, measured dimension, analytical result or clinical outcome is represented.

What the images can tell us

Excipients have functional roles. A visually even mixture is not proof of uniformity. The cutaway is original CGI, not a CT scan; the depth control explores its geometry and does not perform a test.

Disintegration is not dissolution. A tablet can break into particles that have not dissolved. Actual behavior depends on its materials and test conditions. This artwork gives no medication or manufacturing instructions.
