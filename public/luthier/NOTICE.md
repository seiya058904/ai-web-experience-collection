# LUTHIER — The Anatomy of a Violin

LUTHIER follows an unnamed violin from a silent object through material, craft, tension, bow contact and resonance. The photographic-looking plates were generated for the supplied work with OpenAI image generation. They are synthetic illustrations, not photographs, scans or verified reconstructions of a historical instrument.

The procedural geometry, Canvas/SVG fields, narrative and local sound synthesis were authored for this delivery. The model is an artistic structural study. Acoustic contours, displacement and timing are illustrative rather than measured modes. Sound is synthesized locally and stays silent until the visitor enables it; no recording or research-site media is redistributed.

Exact original prompts, hashes and source research are retained in the Collection repository under `provenance/luthier/` and `docs/luthier/`.

The companion [violin-study.glb](models/violin-study.glb) is the supplied export of the original procedural geometry, with its material images embedded. It is not a museum model or instrument scan.
