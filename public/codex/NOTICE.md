# CODEX — sources and licenses

CODEX — The Anatomy of a Book is a user-supplied original digital craft study, imported from `CODEX-The-Anatomy-of-a-Book.zip` (SHA-256 `10f89d184d7a3a9f2098badf90fdb774c90aff0eb829014ca69df2e393b7f74f`). The delivery does not declare a project-wide license for its original source, copy, procedural book or AI-assisted images. This import does not relabel those materials as MIT, CC0 or public domain.

The prologue photograph and material plates are AI-assisted artwork. The live book is the supplied procedural model; its folding, sewing and marbling are artistic interpretations of related binding processes, not manufacturing instructions or numerical material simulations. CODEX contains no audio.

Craft references retained from the supplied colophon include the University of Illinois Preservation Self-Assessment Program, the Metropolitan Museum of Art's marbled-paper discussion and the Getty's making-of-medieval-art material. They inform the interpretation; the artwork is not a scan of an accessioned historical book.

Collection gallery and social images are captures of the actual final live model, with navigation and DOM copy hidden. The gold title belongs to the original book material and remains in the capture. Original concept frames, source PNGs and the unused closed-book GLB export are preserved in scoped repository provenance, not substituted for live geometry.
