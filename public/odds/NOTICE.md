# ODDS — A playground for chance — notices

An ideal independent probability model with replayable pseudorandom samples; the animation is not measured collision physics. The board, paths and trays are drawn in code. The surface texture is an AI-generated material image. NIST and Penn State pages are cited as mathematical references.

No overall application license is declared in the delivery. The delivery does not ship separate font or Lenis notice files; the standard texts are included here for the packages it declares (Manrope OFL-1.1, Lenis MIT).
