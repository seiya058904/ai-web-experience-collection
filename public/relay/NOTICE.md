# RELAY — source, assets and software notices

RELAY is an original browser-local transport sculpture. Its code, SVG sculpture, typography and copy are authored for this work. AI-generated concept studies informed the sculpture and remain design records in `provenance/relay/upstream/docs/concepts/`; they are not deployed.

- Archivo and IBM Plex Mono are locally hosted under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 — `licenses/Archivo-OFL.txt`, `licenses/IBM-Plex-Mono-OFL.txt`.
- GSAP 3.15.0 is used under its standard "no charge" license; Lenis is MIT. Their notices are in `licenses/`.
- The upstream archive declares no overall application license, so none is claimed here.
- RELAY is a demonstration protocol, not TCP, QUIC, BGP or a messaging service. It sends nothing over the network.
