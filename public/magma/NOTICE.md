# MAGMA — Stone Before Stone

An imagined material study across nine volcanic states, supplied for the AI Web Experience Collection. The sequence compresses geological time and compares different compositions and cooling histories; it does not document one specimen's compulsory transformation. Heat, cooling, movement and fracture values are artistic controls, not calibrated physical measurements.

The eighteen runtime WebP plates are original AI-generated artistic reconstructions created for MAGMA. They are not documentary eruption or specimen photographs. The supplied generation prompts, edits, selected/rejected records and asset hashes are preserved in the Collection's scoped provenance. Scientific photographs consulted through USGS and NPS references are not redistributed in this work. The Collection poster and social image are captures of the actual running material renderer.
