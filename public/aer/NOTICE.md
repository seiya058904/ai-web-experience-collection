# AER — Carried by Air — notices

The landscape, people, wind, routes and timing are authored fiction, not weather, navigation or flying instruction. Cinematic images were made with AI; the live flight study, diagrams, controls and keepsake are rendered in code. FAA handbooks are cited as background only.
