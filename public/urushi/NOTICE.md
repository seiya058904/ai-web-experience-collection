# URUSHI — asset notices

URUSHI — Layers of Lacquer is an original digital material study by the supplied URUSHI contributors. Its imagined elliptical vessel is not a museum object, a workshop product or a named artist's work. Coating, curing, layer thickness, roughness and gold deposition are authored visual approximations, rather than measured material data or a workshop recipe.

The original application, GLSL, procedural geometry, model OBJ, procedural texture, renderer-captured chapter stills, prose and documentation retain the supplied [MIT license](LICENSE.txt). Third-party code and fonts retain their separate licenses in the [license inventory](licenses/README.md).

The actual interactive scene and still edition are generated from the original procedural vessel and renderer. The Collection poster/social images are captures of that actual scene. Thirteen supplied AI-generated visual studies and their complete prompts remain source/design records; they are not runtime photographs or slides. Their provenance does not claim exclusive copyright or supersede the image service's terms. No museum photography is distributed.

Latin Bodoni Moda and Manrope font files were preserved from the supplied build because they were the delivery's sole self-hosted copies. URUSHI CJK is the supplied renamed Noto Serif SC subset; its original provenance and OFL text remain available. All model, texture, still, font and legal asset bytes are preserved. The original archive remains outside this repository.

The Collection integration reuses its locked Three.js 0.186.1 and Lenis 1.3.26, keeps one original animation-frame owner, adds base-aware resources and native Collection navigation, and protects asynchronous startup and restoration. The work has no audio. Research links are references opened by the reader; there is no telemetry, account service or upload.
