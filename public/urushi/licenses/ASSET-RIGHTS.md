## AI-generated images

The images in `docs/visual-bible/` were generated specifically for this work
with the built-in image-generation tool. Each selected study has its complete
prompt beside it. They depict an imagined vessel and illustrative material
studies, not documentary photographs of a museum collection or a named
artist's work. They are supplied as project assets. This record identifies
their generation history; it does not claim exclusive copyright in generated
imagery or override the image service's applicable terms.

## Original rendered stills

The files in `public/assets/stills/` are deterministic renders of the original
procedural model and shader, included as the no-WebGL edition. The opening
render is also supplied as `public/assets/surface-black.webp` so the loading
poster and live object share the same shape and light. These rendered assets
are covered by the root MIT license and contain no third-party photography.
