# NIB — Ink Under Pressure

Original source, layout, editorial copy and procedural scene systems were created for the user and delivered as the user's project material. **The delivery grants no additional license for original work.** The Collection's license does not relicense this work.

Eleven runtime material images and eleven Visual Bible studies were made with OpenAI's image-generation tool. They are generated imagery, including refined and separated material layers, rather than photographs of a named pen. The records do not assert exclusive copyright in generated imagery. The Collection poster and social image are captures of the actual NIB Canvas composition using those generated materials, without UI or baked typography.

The application uses authored qualitative flow, meniscus, contact, fiber uptake and stroke geometry. Magnification, air-admission frequency, dimensions and timing serve the artwork; they are not measured engineering values, a calibrated CFD simulation, or claims about every pen, ink or paper. Research images were not incorporated.

The integrated work imports the already locked GSAP 3.15.0 and Lenis 1.3.26, which are byte-identical to the supplied runtime libraries. Their notices remain preserved. Fonts are the original unmodified local Latin variable files.
