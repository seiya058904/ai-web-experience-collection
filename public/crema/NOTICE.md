# CREMA — 9 Bars

An independent eleven-movement study of particles, pressure, porous flow and an espresso shot. The Collection imports the supplied source and preserves its imagery, geometry, chapter copy, controls and original GSAP clock.

- [Original source-code license](LICENSE.txt): MIT applies to original code, with the supplied limitations for generated imagery and separate components.
- [Original attribution](ATTRIBUTION.md) and [software/font license inventory](licenses/README.md). Three.js and Lenis retain MIT terms; GSAP retains its own Standard License; Archivo Variable retains SIL OFL 1.1. The Collection uses its existing locked dependencies.
- Five runtime images in `assets/` are generated artwork. The machine and cup are generated plates, the granule combines generated albedo with authored geometry, and crema combines a generated macro texture with an authored film. They are not photographs of an experiment or manufacturer images. The supplied provider-terms and non-exclusivity qualifications remain in the original license/attribution.
- [Optional original geometry snapshots](models/README.md) and [optional procedural map snapshots](textures/README.md) retain their original bytes and manifests. These GLBs/PNGs are offline exports from the authored source, not measured scans, and are not loaded by the runtime. The supplied READMEs use their original standalone paths; the files are now in the corresponding namespaced Collection directories.
- Flow, drop timing, particle dimensions, extraction color and foam are qualitative interpretations. Nine bar is this work's reference, not a universal prescription. The cited research is not redistributed as images or datasets and its authors do not endorse this work.
- Sound is original local synthesis, silent until activated. No audio recording, microphone, samples or cloud service is involved.

The Collection poster and social image are captures of the actual opening composition with interface text temporarily hidden in the browser. The captured object uses the supplied generated machine plate; a real runtime capture does not imply that the pictured steel machine is entirely procedural.

Source archive identity, all entry hashes, adaptations, omissions, exact prompts and source records remain in the repository's scoped CREMA provenance. Source and optional export license notices remain intact.
