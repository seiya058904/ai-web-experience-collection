# FRAME

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

The supplied brief delegates normal implementation decisions and recommends Vite + TypeScript. This project uses that stack, with DOM controls and pre-rendered deterministic Canvas frames. No account, backend, runtime API, or deployment is required.

## Product Purpose

An original digital stop-motion short in ten chapters. A visitor sees how spacing and pauses change the personality of one small action, then makes and downloads a genuinely different version.

## Users

Visitors exploring an approachable animation story on desktop or mobile. Exact demographics are unspecified.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Twenty-four independent poses, twelve playback beats per second, six extra opening and closing beats.
- Three real spacing variants, two editable holds, full playback, stepping, reset and undo.
- All website text and accessible names in English.
- Ten substantive chapters, nine reversible handoffs, six Hero states, Lenis integration.
- Local PNG storyboard and self-contained HTML film exports reflect the current configuration.
- Source ZIP, build output, actual image-generation records and honest test evidence. No publication, deployment, push, PR or Collection changes.

## Brand Commitments

Boop is the original lavender soft clay block from the provided visual bible: folded upper-right corner, fixed lower-left thumbprint, two black eyes, two arms and two ivory feet. The red block is its small task; the yellow outline is its goal. Mint is the main field. The film is digital clay art, never claimed as physical stop-motion photography.

## Evidence on Hand

The complete supplied execution brief; eight real generated art references and two additional interface studies; independently checked ACMI, Aardman and Lenis primary sources; deterministic playback engine tests. These do not imply browser or finished-art acceptance.

## Product Principles

1. Let the action carry the story without sound.
2. Every visible editing choice changes real frames or real holds.
3. Keep identity, support, contact and lighting coherent.
4. Preserve the complete story on narrow screens and with reduced motion.
5. Downloads remain usable away from this website.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Native controls, keyboard navigation, clear focus, semantic English content, DOM alternatives to Canvas, reduced-motion support, no autoplay, no scroll locks.
