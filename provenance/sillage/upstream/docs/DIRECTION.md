# SILLAGE — production direction

## Authority and scope

The user has already supplied a specific visual world, three candidate briefs, ten scenes and nine handoffs, and delegated ordinary decisions. This project follows that commitment without an extra design-approval round. Generated images are text-free art plates as requested; semantic typography and controls are composed in the page.

## G1 selection

Three independently generated candidates were inspected with the image viewer. A wins: one open asymmetrical hollow is immediately recognizable, with a thin yellow edge, a folded purple plane and a porous brown resting plane. B conceals too much of the identity to lead the story; it remains a useful close study. C creates a strong path but is too continuous to introduce one imprint. The refined A and independent portrait image preserve the upper-left notch.

## First viewport

A restrained fixed wordmark and navigation. On desktop, the refined image fills the artwork field, with a real serif heading and short paragraph in its clean left third; the material takes the visual weight. On mobile, the portrait artwork leads, followed by the heading and controls in normal flow. Heading: “The shape of scent.” The primary action is “Meet the three notes”. The page identifies itself as an original visual interpretation and makes no smell-simulation claim.

## System

- Canvas: ivory #F3EDE1; image backgrounds retain their actual color, with a narrow edge blend only where needed.
- Ink: #29211E; secondary text #65564D; fine rules #CDC1B0.
- Brightness #E7B353, Spice #594051, Warmth #8A6750. Every color has a name and distinct edge/texture.
- Cormorant Garamond regular/medium and italic for display; DM Sans regular/medium for reading and controls. Self-hosted Latin font files. User titles fall back to system fonts as needed.
- Open editorial compositions, simple underlined actions, thin rules, no card grid or glossy UI.
- The UI title, artwork caption and next action are never carried by raster images.

## Rhythm

1. A generous complete imprint and a quiet invitation.
2. A close study and three vocabulary selectors.
3. A shared baseline and three phase comparisons.
4. Large chord composition with real separation/combination.
5. Equal-scale full/subtraction comparison.
6. A continuous near/middle/far trail.
7. Two intimate memories and one unchanged imprint.
8. The richest long panorama with three stages around empty space.
9. A large editable work with three integer-share controls.
10. A spacious comparison, personal title, three-stage print and real PNG export.

## Signature interaction and motion

One persistent hollow links all precise representations. The three shares change contour thickness, folded edge rhythm and relief depth; a zero share removes every colored feature of that vocabulary. Chapter demonstrations stay separate from the personal work. Nine local scroll handoffs evaluate directly from current progress, using continuous composition, separation, scale and clipping, never accumulated event triggers. Text remains in stable document flow. A single manual RAF drives Lenis and the currently visible handoffs; reduced motion uses native scroll and complete static relationships.

## Quality risks to inspect

The critical seams are photographic relief to precise editable artwork, the memory-to-panorama midpoint, and extreme single-component compositions. The review must compare actual images and screenshots. Mobile must retain all ten scenes, all three phases and working controls. Any discrepancy is recorded in the delivery ledger rather than hidden behind a numerical design score.
