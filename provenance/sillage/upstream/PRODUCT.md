# SILLAGE

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the supplied execution brief: Vite, TypeScript, semantic DOM, SVG and Canvas, with Lenis for coordinated scrolling. A local, static project with bundled assets. No accounts, database, analytics or runtime AI service.

## Users and purpose

A visitor without perfume knowledge explores how three original scent metaphors can be described, combined, remembered and left behind. The visitor completes a ten-chapter visual essay, changes a deterministic imprint and takes away a PNG of their own composition.

## Capabilities and constraints

Ten chapters, nine reversible transitions, three integer shares summing to 100, independent subtraction and memory choices, editable title, reset, state restoration, PNG export, keyboard controls, responsive layouts and reduced motion. English interface and original writing throughout. Visuals are artistic interpretations; the site never promises actual smell, chemical simulation or emotional diagnosis.

The supplied brief authorizes routine design and implementation decisions and asks for a runnable source ZIP. It does not authorize hosting, publishing, repository pushes, PRs or releases.

## Brand commitments

SILLAGE — The shape of scent. Three original vocabularies only: Brightness (pomelo peel), Spice (a suggestion of pepper), Warmth (dry wood). Matte ivory, apricot, aubergine and warm brown; restrained editorial typography; one persistent negative-space hollow. No perfume packaging, glass refraction, buildings, gardens, petals, smoke or particle clouds.

## Evidence on hand

The complete user-supplied 01-sillage(1).md is the governing production brief. Generated assets, implementation and test evidence are pending and must not be represented as completed before execution.

## Product principles

- Every chapter adds a distinct relationship and two readable beats.
- The artwork leads; controls remain clear, modest and real.
- The same parameters determine the preview, revisits, ending and export.
- Reduced motion and failed resources retain the full story.
- Record verified outcomes and explicit limits, never invented passes.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Semantic English headings, native links/buttons/ranges/numeric fields, text equivalents for the artwork, visible keyboard focus, no compulsory sound, no forced scroll or timed reading. User-authored titles retain their original language.
