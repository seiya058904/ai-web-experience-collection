### Status vocabulary

Gate statuses use the brief's exact vocabulary: **NOT STARTED / IN PROGRESS / READY TO TEST / PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT TESTED**. **PASS** means only the assertion and conditions stated beside its evidence. **FAIL** records an observed unmet assertion. **BLOCKED** means a required check could not be established with the available environment. **NOT TESTED** identifies an unperformed or unsupported scenario. **IN PROGRESS** identifies an unfinished correction/retest or packaging step; its last observed FAIL remains visible until a replacement retest closes it. Raw reports also use **CAPTURED**, **PASS_MEASURED** and **LAYOUT_REVIEW** for capture or diagnostic outcomes; those are not substitutes for a gate status. Historical evidence is never rewritten to erase a failure.
