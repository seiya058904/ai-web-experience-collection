# PAPERSUN — Hero concept review

Reviewed 2026-11-09. Three distinct calls to the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool returned three PNG images. Each retained original was inspected at its full 1586 × 992 resolution using `view_image`. Exact prompts and disclosed parameters are recorded in `art/concepts/provenance.json`.

## Selected direction: AI-01, the absent circle

The hero's most important geometry is immediately legible: an empty dark cutout in the upper-right sky and one waking amber attic window below it. Blue roof layers have visibly separate thickness and irregular cut edges. A red linen thread gives the eye a route through the lower composition. The left side has enough quiet navy paper for the supplied live English title without covering the aperture or the lamp.

Refinement should retain the aperture's framing, lamp location, dark empty interior, paper stock thickness, raking right light, and left title safety. The handmade rim is visually imperfect but circular in overall silhouette. Exact mathematical sun geometry must remain code-authored rather than relying on generated seams.

## Alternative: AI-02, the desk before the journey

This is a strong character/material reference: Mira has a small original paper puppet face, vermilion coat and visible gold fasteners. The illuminated paper lantern has convincing material depth. The desk's small gold scraps are decorative offcuts, not an acceptable representation of the three story fragments; there are more than three of them. Do not deploy this unmodified image as fragment inventory evidence. It is retained as an alternative concept, not the opening.

## Alternative: AI-03, view from the attic

The attic beams, paper window, city layers and foreground lantern establish a rich proscenium. The sky aperture is appropriately dark. Many illuminated windows weaken the narrative of a single waking lamp, and the large lamp competes with the missing sun; therefore this is a depth/framing reference and not the selected hero.
