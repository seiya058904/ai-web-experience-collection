## Geometry identity

- `src/fragments.ts` owns all three exact closed SVG paths. The repair target and opening have center (100, 100) and radius 86 in a 200 × 200 SVG coordinate system.
- The outer arcs are exact SVG circular arcs, each spanning 120 degrees. Each interior edge is stored once as a seam polyline and read in opposite directions by its neighboring pieces.
- Each shared edge has its own hand-cut notch profile. No chapter should redraw or replace a shape.
- A path can be translated or uniformly scaled together with the full disc. Local piece coordinates must remain unchanged during the lift.
- Seams use the same interior edge coordinates. Their visible stitched treatment remains after assembly and in the ending.
