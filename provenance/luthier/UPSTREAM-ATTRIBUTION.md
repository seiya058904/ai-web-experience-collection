# LUTHIER — Attribution

Prepared **2026-10-07**. The site is an original digital interpretation of an unnamed violin.

## Project contributions

The narrative brief and creative direction were supplied by the user. Scene composition, implementation, original procedural geometry, SVG/Canvas systems, local sound synthesis and project documentation were created for this delivery with OpenAI assistance.

Eight final raster originals and nine selected concept screens were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. Exact prompts, source files and derivatives are recorded in [assets-originals/manifest.json](assets-originals/manifest.json) and [docs/PROVENANCE.md](docs/PROVENANCE.md). They are synthetic illustrations, not historical instrument photographs. The root code license does not assign them an MIT license.

## Scope of interpretation

The model is an artistic structural study. The sound is original procedural synthesis. The acoustic fields are explanatory artwork with deliberately adjusted time and displacement. They are not measured mode shapes, physical measurements, a luthier plan, or a recording of the synthetic instrument.
