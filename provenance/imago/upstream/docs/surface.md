# IMAGO — narrative surface

## Scope and visitor mode

**Mode:** Experience, supported by editorial reading. The primary surface is the ten-chapter life-history journey. A visitor succeeds by understanding the continuity of one life, noticing stage-specific differences, and revisiting a useful observation without losing their place.

The user's explicit execution brief supplies the audience, content, aesthetics, interaction requirements, and autonomy for ordinary design choices. There are no unresolved product questions blocking this surface.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One animal, different bodies. The same chrysalis and suspension point make change legible; observation replaces spectacle.

**OWN-WORLD:** Bone-ivory air, deep-leaf pauses, a jade middle, then wing orange; macro material and quiet Cormorant Garamond/Inter typography. Controls behave like precise captions beside the work.

**STORY:** Begin at the pupa, look back to the egg, follow growth and emergence, then return to the empty attachment. Compare moments without controlling development.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A complete chrysalis hangs on the right at roughly 60–70% usable height. IMAGO and a short introduction occupy the left. The branch, attachment, and bottom remain clear. Portrait imagery independently reserves a lower text zone.

**FORM:** Hero candidate A, selected through the brief's delegated decision process. No random concept seed was used; the three requested Hero candidates were actual generated alternatives.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature observation

The chapter-seven observation table starts in the middle chrysalis stage. Early/middle/late buttons change the image, description, and stage marker together. A side-by-side view provides an accessible comparison without a mandatory drag gesture. The observer's selection persists locally in that chapter but never changes the biological sequence elsewhere.

## Delivery limits

This is local work. No public URL, hosting action, repository push, PR, or Collection modification is part of completion. The archive and verification report are the concrete handoff.
