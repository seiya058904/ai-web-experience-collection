# BACKSTAGE — Product

**A world changes when its cues change.**

BACKSTAGE lets a visitor believe in a miniature world, discover its physical construction and direct a different version of the same short performance. Its story, *A Door for the Moon*, belongs to one red door and one paper lantern: a room opens into a coast, night arrives, and the lantern returns to the warm doorway.

## The experience

Five acts share one continuous stage. Scrolling changes the authored viewpoint and first scenic transformations. Act IV hands three choices to the visitor. The ending preserves those choices instead of resetting the production.

| Act | Visitor’s experience | Essential continuity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Believe | The room already exists on arrival. | The red door and lantern establish the story. |
| The Other Side | Painted depth becomes visible timber, skin and bracing. | The original scene stays in view while the camera changes. |
| Change the World | Walls turn and wave boards rise into a complete sea. | The same flats and props perform the transformation. |
| Call the Cue | Scene, View and Light alter the performance. | One state controller owns the current pose and latest target. |
| Believe Again | The visitor watches their version from the audience. | The chosen scene and light survive the return; the lantern docks at the door. |

## Scope

One miniature theatre, three scenic states and three direct controls. There is no free camera, scenery editor, character AI, account system or external service. Sound is omitted so the model can be watched quietly.

Scene chooses **Room**, **Sea** or **Night**. View explores the finite audience-to-wings arc. Light moves a real sidelight. Reset restores this production’s choices; it leaves the reader’s position intact. A new Scene choice replaces the pending target from the current safe mechanism pose.

## Interaction principles

- **Continuity is the subject.** A door, lamp, rail or panel is never replaced merely to make a transition convenient.
- **Hold before handing off.** Each act settles into a complete composition before the next reveal.
- **Controls have visible consequences.** Scene moves geometry, View changes occlusion, and Light changes illumination and shadows.
- **Return is reversible.** The IV-to-V handoff reads an immutable snapshot and scroll progress. Its structural motion stops when scrolling stops and retraces the same path when reversed.
- **The story survives a weaker device.** Still mode retains the controls; the static edition retains the narrative and composed views while clearly removing unavailable real-time controls.

## Delivery

The product is delivered as complete local source with a lockfile, local assets, English installation instructions, research and provenance, state tests and an actual acceptance record. Public deployment and modification of existing collections are outside this delivery.
