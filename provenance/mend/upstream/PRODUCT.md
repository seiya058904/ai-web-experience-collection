# MEND — Still Yours

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the supplied execution brief: a lightweight Vite + TypeScript application, Lenis, and a single shared Three.js toy model with chapter-specific static alternatives. No server, account, paid service, runtime API, or telemetry.

## Product Purpose

An original ten-chapter interactive short film about helping an old wooden pull-along dog take part in life again while preserving the marks that make it someone's own.

## Users

Visitors reading and exploring on desktop and mobile, with complete keyboard and reduced-motion alternatives.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Ten effective chapters, nine reversible handovers, three inspection points, two feasible fictional repair choices, a controlled before/after trial, aligned comparison, a full sunlit journey, and a truthful final care record. Choice A keeps the original rear wheel and adds a visible sleeve. Choice B replaces that wheel and returns the original in a bag. Only B has an old wheel beside Pip. Repair choice is independent of story progress.

All authored website text is English. Characters are Pip, Lan, and He. All characters, letters, and repair mechanisms are fictional. This is a digital story, not real toy repair or toy safety advice.

## Brand Commitments

Original cream-yellow wooden Pip; two cobalt-blue front wheels and two natural-wood rear wheels; red pulling cord; chipped right ear; worn paint; small orange sun on the underside; exactly three move marks. Warm sunlit workbench, restrained serif headings and clear sans-serif body copy. No anthropomorphic movement.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied 07-mend(1).md is the complete execution brief. New image generation, browser checks, source and build artifacts will be recorded with actual evidence. No previous assets or testing results are claimed.

## Product Principles

- Understand a mark before deciding to change it.
- Make choices visible in the toy, the returned parts, and the care record.
- Keep the same Pip through every chapter.
- A short completed walk earns the ending.
- Deliver a reproducible local source ZIP without deploying, pushing, publishing, or changing the Collection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic English DOM, native keyboard controls, visible focus, mobile equivalents, sufficient contrast, responsive reading order, no scroll locking, and complete reduced-motion and graphics-failure alternatives.
