# URUSHI — Layers of Lacquer

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Implementation choice within the user's explicitly delegated medium strategy: a static Vite application with authored WebGL material rendering and Lenis as the sole smooth-scroll authority. No account, API, backend, remote runtime asset or hosting service is required. The finished build is delivered in the ZIP alongside source.

## Product Purpose

An immersive digital artwork showing one original wooden vessel acquiring depth through ground, thin lacquer coats, humid curing, abrasion, repeated layers, polishing, and late maki-e. The visitor should feel a surface being made.

## Users

Desktop and mobile visitors who explore by scrolling. The artifact is an experience, not a shop, craft tutorial, cultural encyclopedia or product campaign.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve one continuous object and an evolving long reflection.
- Preserve layer accumulation, humidity curing, abrasion, polish, depth and late gold.
- Immediate input response with patient, surface-bound visual motion.
- No explosions, fast orbits, assembly, technical HUD, decorative Japanese clichés or early black-and-gold branding.
- Responsive mobile crops; bounded rendering resolution and particle counts.
- Deliver ZIP only: source, final assets, AI studies, model, textures, shaders, README, build instructions, licenses and provenance. No deployment and no Sites.

## Brand Commitments

URUSHI — Layers of Lacquer. Surface becoming depth. Black leads, vermilion is a distinct turn, gold gains weight only late. Quiet, tactile, precise, contemporary and museum-grade.

## Evidence on Hand

Research from Japan Kogei Association, Wajima Museum of Urushi Art and museum conservation/collection resources. Images and the vessel in this work are original illustrative digital assets, not historical collection documentation. Conditions, thicknesses and elapsed time shown visually are not a production recipe.

## Product Principles

1. Material quality outweighs realtime purity.
2. The surface grows deeper as the visual system grows quieter.
3. Research informs the sequence without turning it into a universal workshop method.
4. Responsive composition preserves the material rather than shrinking the desktop frame.
5. Every shipped asset has an explicit source and license record.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation defaults: semantic reading order, keyboard-accessible chapter navigation, reduced-motion support, explicit render fallback and legible mobile contrast.
