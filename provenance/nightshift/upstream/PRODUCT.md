# NIGHTSHIFT

## Confirmed brief

A complete English-language browser-native editorial experience, set in fictional Meridian City. Eleven chapters follow parallel night workers from midnight to first service. The visitor should notice the preparation behind an ordinary morning. The user supplied a precise execution brief and authorized ordinary design and implementation decisions without an approval pause.

## Audience and use

People who enjoy humane documentary cinema and urban observation, on desktop and mobile. Visitors control the pace through native page scrolling, chapter links, and accessible small interactions. Sound begins muted. No account, API, database, analytics, live statistics, or public deployment.

## Pinned commitments

The supplied ink, teal, amber, rose and morning palette; an anonymous human figure in a single lit window; ten editorial light/architecture handoffs; real generated photographic art; restrained English DOM typography; all eleven chapters and their two content beats; a matched return to the original window at dawn; Lenis; reproducible source ZIP and evidence.

## Delivery

Private local development and testing. Complete portable source, licensed local assets, lockfile, Windows and cross-platform commands, truthful acceptance records, and extraction/rebuild verification. No publishing or changes to other projects.

## Implementation decisions

Use layered photography and precise SVG diagrams instead of fragile character 3D. The brief explicitly permits this fidelity fallback. Use a single deterministic scroll evaluator, native flow for all reading content, and opaque scene-owned transition regions. These are implementation choices, not user-provided factual claims.
