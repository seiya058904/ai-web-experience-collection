## Type and tokens

- Display: locally hosted Barlow Condensed, medium/semibold; urban lettering with natural English case and intentional line breaks. Hero scale may exceed the generic skill floor because the pinned cinematic brief requires a directed large opening.
- Reading and controls: locally hosted DM Sans, 400/500. Main body is 16–19px, line-height 1.55–1.7, with short editorial measures.

- Midnight ink `#0B1421`, steel teal `#203545`, working amber `#E9A85F`, predawn rose `#DDA6A0`, washed morning `#E5EAE9`.
- Text on dark `#F0EEE7`; muted dark text surfaces `#BDC8CC`. Dark reading panels are opaque enough for reliable contrast.
- Fine rules and squared editorial panels; no generic card grid. Controls use consistent 1.5px authored SVG icons.

## Media and compositing

Final art ships as native-dimension WebP with separate small fallback files. No arbitrary global color filter is applied. Source-specific photograph exposure, adjacent ink/paper panels and bounded edge shading protect typography. Images remain static at reading holds; ambient details, if enabled, are minor, optional and paused in the background.

This is a layered photographic experience, not fully rotatable 3D. Native HTML reading blocks remain in document flow. A fixed photographic stage draws at most source, target and the special AI-08 midpoint. Opaque clip regions meet behind a mullion, frame, curb, pipe or column. No unrelated people share a translucent dissolve.

AI-08 is the visible rail-to-water midpoint. It is an editorial match between separate facilities, never a shared plumbing claim. The atlas and explanatory diagrams are authored SVG geometry, not generated pictures of interfaces.
