# VAREN

## Platform
Web. Responsive local-first concept menswear showcase.

## Audience and purpose
Professionals exploring high-end men's tailoring. Browse six concept looks, inspect garment imagery, filter the collection, and save a personal selection.

## Confirmed commitments
Original concept brand VAREN; English interface. Warm tailoring catalogue aesthetic chosen by the user: limewash paper, deep umber ink, cool slate accents, fine serif typography, generous imagery. Hero: “Presence, precisely cut.” Primary action: “Explore the collection”.

Images are AI-generated concept menswear, not goods offered for sale. No invented prices, material certifications, testimonials, business history, or store locations. No backend, checkout, or publication in this delivery.

## Stack
Technology choice delegated and plan approved: React, Vite, TypeScript, GSAP, @gsap/react, Lenis. Single GSAP ticker drives Lenis. Desktop smooth wheel; native touch; reduced-motion support. Filters and look details reflected in URL; saved looks persist locally.

## Delivery
Complete locally runnable source and assets. Deployment requires separate authorization.
