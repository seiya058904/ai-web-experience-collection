# VAREN: a contemporary tailoring catalogue

## Authority
The user chose warm tailoring editorial and an English interface, then requested a more modern, less classical expression. This explicit commitment overrides concept-seed a56b38be. The contemporary concept replaces the first classical hero: no statues, arches, antique styling, ornate framing, or classical serif wordmark.

## World
Warm limestone paper, umber ink, a restrained cool slate signal. Sentient 300 contemporary roman serif for display and 400 for product names; Hanken Grotesk for body, labels, navigation, and VAREN wordmark. Local WOFF2 fonts. Two families, square photographs, open product layouts, fine rules. No grain/noise overlays, gradient washes, commercial claims, or invented brand heritage.

## Composition and content
Six chapters: campaign hero, six-look collection, typographic silhouette study, dark garment-detail chapter, occasion wardrobe selector, dark wordmark footer. Twelve-column capped canvas; max 1440px including desktop gutters. Hero 44/56 text/photo split, photograph prominent, small lapel inset, headline/one sentence/outlined CTA. Mobile becomes heading, action, portrait, then collection. No new above-fold copy beyond the approved concept.

## Interaction
One outlined button family and understated text links. Product details, saved looks, and navigation use a native modal dialog with visible close control and focus restoration. URL stores collection filter, current look, and saved view. Local storage stores valid look IDs. Bookmark controls have pressed state and descriptive accessible names.

## Motion
Single GSAP ticker drives Lenis. lerp 0.1; smoothWheel true; syncTouch false; native touch. One short hero settle, one pinned detail sequence on desktop, state-specific transitions. No mouse parallax, custom cursor, repeated reveal choreography, or permanent loops. Reduced motion disables smoothing and pinning. All animations and listeners cleaned up on unmount, including development StrictMode remounts.

## Hallmark deviations
Custom adaptation of dealt Atelier after the user's modernity correction. Theme draw/scaffold for atelier fail on installed theme parsing (no header block); custom scaffold used, then palette/type set explicitly. Reflex 83 is waived: six garment looks are the requested product content, and detail imagery must remain legible rather than be replaced by filler typography. No WebGL ambient field, sample blotters, or paper fibre: the user explicitly asked for a more modern expression.

## Verification
Build/type checks, Hallmark static/render attempt, Impeccable detector, six-width overflow checks, desktop/mobile screenshots, keyboard/focus, filter/detail/back, local persistence, Lenis integration and simulated scroll traces. Physical touchpad and real mobile smoothness require actual hardware and are not inferred from automation.
