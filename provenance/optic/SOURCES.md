# Engineering references

Research date: **2026-10-06**.

These primary sources informed OPTIC's engineering vocabulary and visual mechanisms. They are references, not asset suppliers. No manufacturer's product photograph, diagram, logo, CAD model or video is redistributed in this project. OPTIC's camera and component geometry were authored for this experience. The named organizations do not sponsor or endorse it.

| Source                                                                                                                                                                | What it informed                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Edmund Optics — Introduction to Basic Ray Optics](https://www.edmundoptics.com/knowledge-center/application-notes/optics/introduction-to-basic-ray-optics/)          | Refraction at material boundaries, surface normals, Snell's law, and the distinct behavior of positive and negative lens elements.                                                                                                        |
| [Edmund Optics — System Throughput, f/#, and Numerical Aperture](https://www.edmundoptics.com/knowledge-center/application-notes/imaging/lens-iris-aperture-setting/) | Effective aperture diameter, relative light throughput, and why doubling the f-number reduces ideal aperture area to one quarter.                                                                                                         |
| [Edmund Optics — Depth of Field and Depth of Focus](https://www.edmundoptics.com/knowledge-center/application-notes/imaging/depth-of-field-and-depth-of-focus/)       | Defocus cones, the relationship between aperture and depth of field, and the distinction between scene depth and sensor-plane alignment.                                                                                                  |
| [Nikon — NIKKOR Z 50mm f/1.4](https://imaging.nikon.com/imaging/lineup/lens/z-mount/z_50mmf14/)                                                                       | A credible reference class for a 50 mm f/1.4–f/16 prime with nine rounded aperture blades. OPTIC does not reproduce this lens's seven-group construction or optical prescription.                                                         |
| [Canon — Electronic shutter vs mechanical shutter](https://www.canon-europe.com/pro/infobank/electronic-vs-mechanical-shutter/)                                       | Two focal-plane curtains traveling in the same direction; whole-gate exposure at slower settings and a traveling slit at faster settings. Older product-era statements elsewhere in the article are not adopted as current market claims. |
| [Nikon — Z 6 specifications](https://imaging.nikon.com/imaging/lineup/mirrorless/z_6/)                                                                                | A coherent full-frame mirrorless architecture combining CMOS capture, focal-plane phase-detection pixels, an image processor and sensor-shift stabilization. OPTIC's numerical specification is independently defined.                    |
| [Canon Science Lab — CMOS Sensors](https://global.canon/en/technology/s_labo/light/003/05.html)                                                                       | Photodiodes accumulating charge under illumination and the subsequent conversion to electrical signals.                                                                                                                                   |
| [Nikon's MicroscopyU — Color Balance in Digital Imaging](https://www.microscopyu.com/digital-imaging/color-balance-in-digital-imaging)                                | Monochromatic photosite measurements beneath color filters; Bayer sampling with two green filters for every red and blue filter.                                                                                                          |
| [Nikon — What is Vibration Reduction?](https://www.nikon.com.mt/en_MT/learn-and-explore/magazine/gear/vibration-reduction)                                            | Detection and compensation of pitch, yaw, roll, horizontal and vertical camera motion through sensor movement.                                                                                                                            |
| [Sony — ILCE-9 specifications and features](https://www.sony.co.uk/electronics/interchangeable-lens-cameras/ilce-9/specifications)                                    | A credible magnesium internal frame, separate external covers, full-frame sensor packaging and integrated camera electronics. No Sony industrial design is reproduced.                                                                    |

## Interpretation

The camera is an original explanatory design. Published principles inform the directions of movement and the order of the optical/electronic path. They do not validate OPTIC as a manufacturable camera, optimized lens prescription or calibrated optical instrument. See [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md) for the implementation's deliberate visual simplifications.
