# VEIL — Fabric in Motion

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Implementation choice within the requested technical direction: TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, GSAP/ScrollTrigger and Lenis. This is a browser-local, static experience, with no service or account dependencies.

## Product Purpose

An original immersive digital installation in which scrolling turns thread into textile, textile into folds, and folds into a luminous spatial form. The artwork itself is the interface. It is not a store, fashion brand site or lookbook.

## Users

Visitors exploring materials and motion on desktop or mobile. The explicit acceptance devices are 1080p, 1440p, 4K and mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every main scene has a purpose-built geometry, textile, light or motion system.
- One continuous material narrative with enter, stable composition, living hold, handoff and exit.
- Smooth forward and reverse scroll, direct chapter navigation, motion control, touch support and reduced-motion support.
- All runtime resources are local and included in the delivery.
- Deliver one complete runnable ZIP with source, build configuration, production output, instructions, attribution and licenses. Do not publish or create a hosted preview.

## Brand Commitments

VEIL — Fabric in Motion. Elegant, tactile, airy, luminous, precise, cinematic and experimental. Warm ivory, charcoal, silver grey and restrained warm neutrals. Sparse English exhibition copy is an implementation assumption based on the supplied English title and chapter names.

## Product Principles

- The material creates the next scene.
- High-quality imagery supports an authored world of geometry and light.
- Complete compositions must have time to be seen.
- Graceful operation matters across devices and motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic readable scene copy, keyboard chapter navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, motion pause and prefers-reduced-motion support. WebGL failure must preserve a complete readable experience.
