# CREMA — 9 Bars

## Product truth
An immersive, local-only web artwork following hot water through a dry porous coffee bed, saturation, pressure, percolation, extraction, first drop, stream, crema and a tiny espresso. The visitor explores a material transformation through scroll, not a shop or a tutorial.

## Audience and success
For visitors who enjoy ambitious digital art, material cinematography and precision machines. The first viewport must make the visitor feel pressure waiting to release. The defining interaction is a fully reversible macro-to-micro journey whose innumerable flow paths finally converge into the opening suspended drop.

## Explicit commitments
Cold polished stainless steel against warm liquid; dark granular grounds, not sand; matte dry versus darker reflective wet material; spatial pressure, a restrained single 9 BAR moment; irregular porous paths; gas-in-liquid crema with oil dispersed in the aqueous phase. No cafe, lifestyle hands, flying beans, latte art or brown template.

Lenis is the only smooth scrolling authority, synchronized with GSAP and the render loop. Desktop and mobile keep the whole story. GPU work and device pixel ratio are bounded. Reverse scroll reconstructs material state. Local development, build and QA only. No Sites, hosting, public preview, git publication or deployment.

## Deliverable
One ZIP containing complete source, final optimized assets, accepted AI production assets, procedural model/shader/texture sources, build output once, README, build instructions, licenses, attribution and provenance. Exclude dependencies, cache, traces, test junk, rejected generations and duplicate builds.

## Decisions delegated by the user
Creative autonomy is explicit. The detailed brief settles purpose, route, constraints, visual world and success criteria, so no additional confirmation round is needed. English editorial interface is inferred from the English project title, scene names and requested copy. A Chinese quick start will accompany the ZIP.
