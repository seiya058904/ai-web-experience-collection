# PAS · Visual Bible

## Direction

A quiet, editorial movement work. Bodies occupy the visual field; the interface lives at its edge. The user's supplied brief is the visual authority. An image-first process produced eight real images before production scene and layout code.

## Tokens

Charcoal brown #27221F; pale clay #C98F75; sand #C6B7A3; Cen red #963F32; soft white #F2E8DA. Broad soft side light, matte plaster and cotton; contact shadows, no bloom or neon.

Typography: Bodoni Moda for display and Manrope for UI/body, self-hosted with system fallback. Body 16px; small UI 12–14px with explicit line-height. Titles use natural English line breaks. The large PAS wordmark is deliberate opening typography, outside both bodies.

## Composition

Desktop: left reading margin and dominant right/central full-body field. Mobile: title above an independent portrait opening; live scene keeps both bodies in a compact diagonal arrangement. Controls follow captions in normal document flow. A sparse bottom chapter rail communicates sequence. No cards, rankings, dashboards or decorative metrics.

## Movement

One normalized narrative coordinate controls bodies, contact, camera and copy. Native scroll remains available. Lenis has manual RAF only. Independent playback explicitly owns the local position until input or Exit returns it to scroll. No accumulated bone positions or event-only chapter transitions.

## Image use

IMG-01 establishes the horizontal full-body route; IMG-02 is a high-angle alternative; IMG-03 supplies hand study. IMG-04 is the refined opening; IMG-05 is an independently composed mobile image; IMG-06, 07 and 08 guide counterweight, the complete phrase and the turn after crossing. Generated stills are art references and opening assets, not evidence of feasible biomechanics.
