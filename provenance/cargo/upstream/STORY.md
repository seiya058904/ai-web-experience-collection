# CARGO — The same box, a changing world

## Complete offline story

One fictional container connects eleven moments of a journey. This text preserves both narrative beats of every chapter, descriptions of the visible scenes and interaction outcomes, and all ten handoffs with their five stages. It can be read without JavaScript, a graphics processor or an internet connection.

CARGO / BOX-001 is a fictional exhibit identity. The ports, route, operator, seal and cargo are invented. The standard 20-foot nominal external envelope is 6.058 × 2.438 × 2.591 m; precise mechanism details are schematic. [Sources and interpretation](docs/SOURCES.md) explain the distinction.
