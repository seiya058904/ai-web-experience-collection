# CARGO — Visual Bible

## Locked direction

An industrial photographic essay, interrupted by precise, tactile interface studies. The user-specified direction is binding: monumental machinery, a quiet red container, cool dock fog, warm light from camera right, and recognizable physical contact. The supplied gantry concept A is selected. Concept B supplies a close object reference; concept C supplies intermodal order. Alternatives are retained as compressed evidence, not mixed into the opening.

The concept-seed utility was run. Its remote catalog was unavailable. The brief already pins the photographic world and ordinary design decisions are delegated; no new direction approval was introduced. The user's direction takes precedence over a random alternative.

## Image roles and treatments

- AI-04: desktop opening, complete crane on the right, single grounded box below, quiet left sky. Full photographic color, no colored wash. A small neutral left-edge veil is allowed only when contrast measurement requires it.
- AI-05: separately generated portrait opening. Tall crane leg at the right, quiet upper sky, substantial complete container at the bottom. Never obtained by cropping AI-04.
- AI-06: principal lift, cables and attached spreader clearly visible. Read white type on the naturally dark left; bounded neutral scrim only behind text.
- AI-07: sea, deliberate distance. The right-side vessel and actual red deck position stay intact.
- AI-08: grounded/engaged midpoint design reference, opaque beam proves depth ordering. Realtime mechanism is intentionally a labeled schematic, not a claim to be a photographic simulation.
- Additional road, rail, yard and receiving-port images give the middle and late chapters independent environments. Separate framing positions retain the relevant container and supporting vehicle on narrow screens.

All photographs are generated fictional composite illustrations. Their geometry is an artistic reference, not a manufacturing specification. The reusable measured model is the authoritative object for dimensions, door pivots, packed contents and support continuity. Precise markings and labels are real DOM/SVG, never baked image lettering.

## Typography and layout

Self-hosted Barlow Condensed 600/700 for large industrial editorial headings; self-hosted variable Archivo for prose and controls. Generous short measure, sentence case, no image text and no tiny data costume. A restrained wordmark, chapter access, motion preference and exhibit notes form the top navigation. The eleven-step rail is wayfinding, not a shipping dashboard. Metadata numbers correspond only to chapter order or verified dimensions.

The opening at 1440 × 900 places the title in the left 40%, box in lower right, gantry across the upper right, navigation on a quiet top line. At 390 × 844 the title moves into the portrait image's upper fog and controls remain in document flow. Technical chapters place the model alongside the copy on wide screens and below it on phones. Photographic chapters vary heading measure, horizon placement and inset reading-strip density.

## Motion grammar

One Lenis instance and one GSAP ticker. One deterministic state evaluator maps current scroll position to the active chapter and boundary, mechanical state, camera transforms, occluder and captions. Scene transitions are unlagged functions of progress. No intro scroll lock. Camera travel is restrained; the long rail view pulls back, doors pivot, the crane engages before lifting, and carrier changes occur behind actual opaque structural shapes. The stage is explicitly a conceptual interface study. No translucent duplicate container handoffs.

The ten boundary sequences have five documented stages. Every boundary keeps one canonical actor, a specific anchor and a target state. Mechanical captions occupy their own stable reading area; outgoing and incoming prose never share coordinates. Still mode presents the complete target plate and retains stage selection, content and interaction. Hidden/unchanged WebGL does not continuously redraw.

## Identity contract

One 20-foot standard external envelope: 6.058 × 2.438 × 2.591 m. Two doors and two locking rods. Upper-right diagonal door scratch and fictional CARGO / BOX-001 identity. Six authored timber packing blocks have one fixed arrangement in both departure and arrival. x is length, y is up, z is width; the rear doors occupy positive x. Fine fitting details, hardware and interior wall thickness are schematic, without certification claims.

## Purposeful exceptions

Technical study graphics are deliberately measured diagrammatic views rather than a weak imitation of the generated photographs. They make inaccessible interiors and carrier relationships observable. Model and image moments never overlap as translucent copies. No audio samples are shipped; the sound control explains this clearly. Physical shipping timings, real routes, live location and emissions are omitted.
