# BERTH

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the supplied execution brief: Vite, TypeScript, semantic HTML, CSS and Lenis. No framework is needed for this compact original story. Dependencies and fonts are pinned and bundled.

## Product Purpose
A complete, original, ten-chapter English night-train story. The reader accompanies Shen Ning, 29, to her first community writing class in fictional Linwan, sharing a compartment with Ms. Lin, 61, on her first solo journey to the sea after retirement.

## Users
People reading an intimate fictional journey on a desktop or mobile browser. No account or specialist knowledge is required.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Ten full chapters, nine reversible transitions, first-line editing and local-only saving, a chapter-three-only lamp comparison, optional ambient audio, a motion pause, reduced-motion equivalence, full keyboard access and responsive compositions. No remote data service, telemetry, paid runtime API or deployment. Deliver runnable source ZIP with assets, Windows instructions and honest test evidence.

## Brand Commitments
BERTH; understated humane English narration; a single fixed compartment; two ordinary travellers; cream cotton, old walnut, midnight blue, warm brass light and one orange-red paper clip. No melodrama or inspirational marketing.

## Accessibility
English document language; readable flow text; real DOM text on notes; editable input preserved in its original language; keyboard controls; readable dark states; optional animation and audio; full story even when images fail.

## Evidence
Supplied 03-berth(1).md. Original generated imagery and generation records in docs/asset-records.json. Official Nightjet facilities research informed the shared small-space layout without copying a real train. No existing repository was modified.
