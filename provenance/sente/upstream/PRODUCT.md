# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated explicitly by the supplied execution brief: a lightweight reproducible frontend, preferably Vite + TypeScript. Selected vanilla TypeScript + Vite, semantic DOM and exact SVG, with Lenis as the only motion dependency. No account, backend, telemetry, external runtime API, or deployment.

## Users

People encountering Go through an original, short interactive story, on desktop or mobile. They need to see what a move changes before learning wider strategic claims.

## Product Purpose

SENTE is a ten-chapter playable story about liberties, connection, capture, and considering one reply. Three finite original puzzles use exact seven-by-seven boards. Success is understanding visible local consequences; there is no score, rank, timer, or invented playing strength.

## Operating Context

An English-only website with scrolling for narrative and explicit clicks or keyboard actions for moves. All ten chapters are available without solving any puzzle. Choices remain independent from scroll direction. A local SVG observation note can be downloaded at the end.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ten effective chapters; nine reversible adjacent transitions.
- All positions follow the exact supplied coordinates. A1 is the bottom-left point, G7 the top-right; edges are actual boundaries.
- Genuine capture, orthogonal connection, deduplicated liberties, suicide rejection, and the K1 immediate-ko example.
- Three reversible one-move exercises; P3 uses a stated scripted white response.
- Eight actual generated art outcomes before production layout, with recorded selection and provenance.
- Production SVG supplies every authoritative intersection, stone, coordinate and rule result.
- Source ZIP, formal assets, lockfile, Windows instructions, actual checks and explicit limits.
- Do not publish, deploy, push, create a PR/Release, or merge into an existing collection.

## Brand Commitments

Name SENTE. Warm wood, charcoal black, soft white, dark brown and one muted cinnabar accent. Real stone weight, soft upper-left side light, careful emptiness. English serif titles and stable sans-serif body. No borrowed cultural props or sweeping life metaphors.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied `05-sente(1).md` contains all ten chapters, exact puzzle coordinates, transition contracts and acceptance matrix. `docs/research.md` and `docs/reference-enumeration.json` provide independent rule-source and puzzle evidence. The nine actual image outputs, prompts and adoption decisions are recorded in `art/generation-log.json`.

## Product Principles

1. Rules commit before animation.
2. Every local claim has a visible board explanation.
3. Scrolling never plays a move.
4. Personal records remain truthful, including failed attempts and unplayed examples.
5. Complete interaction survives reduced motion or failed decorative resources.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic headings, native controls, English accessible names, coordinate fallback, arrow-key grid navigation, visible focus, touch input, reduced motion, optional muted-by-default sound and no required dragging.
