# PELAGIC

An independent browser exhibition: **A Language of Living Light**.

The visitor changes the conditions of observation, inspects a reconstructed encounter, and compares squid pigmentation. Five observations form one reversible narrative. Three animals are presented in separate observation settings. This is an artistic, research-informed interpretation, not a recorded dive, photometric simulation, translation of animal signals, or institutional exhibition.

## Deliverable

Local source ZIP only. No deployment, remote push, repository changes, accounts, analytics, telemetry, or external media required at runtime. English interface and README. Include research, original assets, image generation provenance, design decisions, and measured QA evidence.

## Core interactions

1. Survey illumination is separate from the lure's own emission. First input enters manual mode; only Restore authored view exits it.
2. One deterministic fish encounter has an independently stored playback position, pause, replay and scrubbing. Scrolling never restarts it.
3. Two research-grounded squid pigmentation patterns have a separate conceptual backlight switch and a still comparison.

Motion can be paused globally. Reduced-motion retains the work and controls. Real text and native inputs remain readable at all illumination settings. No audio is required or included.
