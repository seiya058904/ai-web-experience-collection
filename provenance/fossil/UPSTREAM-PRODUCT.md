# FOSSIL — Deep Time in Stone

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Implementation choice for this delivery: portable static HTML/CSS and modular JavaScript, locally bundled Lenis as the sole global smooth-scroll authority, Canvas and a local WebGL renderer where appropriate. No framework or deployment target was mandated by the user.

## Users
Visitors exploring an immersive, museum-quality work on fossil evidence through deliberate scrolling. Exact audience demographics are unspecified.

## Product Purpose
Make the invisible past readable: life becomes evidence and geological time is held in stone. The requested narrative is Burial → Preservation → Exposure → Scan → Reconstruction → Archive.

## Positioning
An immersive deep-time archive joining geology, specimen preparation and scientific imaging. The specimen leads; facts and interpretation follow.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Ten scenes: Specimen, Strata, Burial, Preservation, Exposure, Amber, Scan, Reconstruct, Archive, Deep Time. Shared strata and contour systems; cautious preparation, CT slices and incomplete volume reconstruction. Reversible scrolling, responsive mobile, reduced motion, bounded GPU use. ZIP only, with source, production assets, AI originals, models, procedural/scan data, README, licenses and provenance. No OpenAI Sites and no public deployment.

## Brand Commitments
FOSSIL — Deep Time in Stone. Museum × geology × sculpture × scientific imaging. Limestone, bone ivory, shale, museum black, iron brown, muted umber, honey amber and restrained cool scan grey. Extreme specimen scale and museum lighting. Curatorial, quiet camera language. No prehistoric fantasy, dinosaur entertainment or medical HUD.

## Evidence on Hand
No real accessioned specimen or measured CT volume supplied. All commissioned imagery and generated specimen/scan data must be identified as interpretive or synthetic. Primary scientific sources will support general mechanisms; no invented collecting locality, specimen age, museum accession or scientific measurement.

## Product Principles
- Let the object establish itself before explaining it.
- Let layers and contours carry the scene handoffs.
- Preserve uncertainty in reconstruction.
- Keep every stable composition readable and exhibit-like.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation and focus visibility; readable text; no dependence on hover or precise pointer input; reduced-motion support; mobile simplification that retains all ten story beats.
