# CHRYSALIS

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

The supplied build brief specifies Vite + TypeScript, real HTML/CSS text, precise SVG diagrams, real Lenis, and generated macro art. React and Three.js are optional. This implementation uses vanilla TypeScript and layered stills because the illustrated biological stages must remain clear, fast, deterministic, and reviewable.

## Users

Curious visitors and nature learners following one illustrated monarch butterfly, Danaus plexippus, through its life cycle.

## Product Purpose

An eleven-chapter, browser-native narrative about the architecture of becoming. Visitors follow an egg, milkweed habitat, hatchling, feeding larva, five instars, J-shaped preparation, chrysalis, development, late wing pigmentation, emergence, and first flight.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The user supplied complete English copy, eight image-generation assignments, ten transition contracts, scientific evidence requirements, accessibility alternatives, and an extraction/rebuild delivery gate. All eleven chapters and all ten transitions are mandatory. All UI and documentation must be English. No login, paid runtime API, analytics, backend, or publication. Local source ZIP is the deliverable. No collection edits, remote Git activity, or hosting operations are authorized.

## Brand Commitments

Scientific macro nature cinema with a restrained museum editorial layer. Leaf green, jade, eggshell, fine gold, copper and black are fixed by the brief. Real selectable text. A consistent leaf vein and a later silk attachment connect the illustrated scenes. Internal diagrams are labeled explanatory reconstructions. No magical morphing, particles, invented measurements or misleading transparent biology.

## Evidence on Hand

The original CHRYSALIS Edition 2.0 brief. Primary sources reviewed on November 9, 2026: U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service and Monarch Joint Venture. Generated images and their exact prompts are recorded in the art provenance manifest. No scientific institution endorsement or photographic provenance is claimed.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the complete story in every supported viewing mode.
- Separate scientific claims from authored visual interpretations.
- Make the image and content state reproducible from current scroll position.
- Every visible control has a real, reversible result.
- Report measured evidence and explicit limitations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Native navigation, focusable headings, semantic buttons, keyboard arrow navigation for stage selectors, visible focus, responsive typography, complete reduced-motion and still views, silent default, readable asset-error states, and mobile compositions are required. No claim of formal WCAG conformance is made.
