# Collection design contract

## Overview

Experience mode. Nine finished works are the content. A cool, pale gallery frame creates room for each work's own visual world. No ordinary detached project cards, badge stacks, ornamental new scene or loading gate.

## Colors

Gallery tokens are above. The images and each work's established typography carry the nine distinct identities. Dark or light overlays protect each work's entrance text; they do not recolor the works themselves.

## Typography

Barlow for the quiet gallery voice; Barlow Condensed for the racing and space titles; VERDANT's supplied Instrument Serif for its secondary entrance typography. All are local. The opening invitation is plain, short and readable.

## Layout

One restrained header and invitation sit above three edge-to-edge image triptychs, separated by a 3px seam. Each entire panel is a native link. At 700px and below, the panels form a vertical sequence with independent, substantial visual compositions. Footer holds credits. No horizontal scrolling or hidden navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Photographs supply depth. Optional pointer movement shifts the existing image by a few pixels; it neither runs a perpetual clock nor loads any work's rendering engine. Reduced motion removes it.

## Shapes

Square media, thin gallery rules, a simple three-stroke collection mark and directional line icons. No decorative card shells.

## Components

The entrance is functional without JavaScript. Focus remains visible. Work headers expose a shared arrow-and-Collection return link, reduced to a labeled arrow on narrow screens; each header's existing colors remain authoritative. Menus and work controls are unchanged.

## Do's and Don'ts

Preserve the independent work styles. Reuse their actual assets, not recreated thumbnails. Do not import WebGL, React or GSAP on the homepage. Do not add extra imagery, a mandatory transition/loading sequence, or a competing continuous clock. Full document navigation releases each work's runtime.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a single nine-world visual entrance, with the works themselves as the first viewport's substance.
OWN-WORLD: pale gallery, sharp photographic edges, racing condensed type, botanical serif and the planetary horizon.
STORY: recognize nine distinct experiences, choose one, explore, return.
FIRST VIEWPORT: quiet header and invitation above a dominant three-panel photographic composition; Explore remains visible at each lower corner.
FORM: three code-led triptychs using media from the actual works; existing production assets are the reference.
FINISH: desktop/mobile full-frame review, verified native entry/return links, documented tokens and retained source provenance. Review stays in this chat as required by repository instructions; no additional agents.

GLAZE adds its supplied Archivo voice, celadon jar and pale green ground. KAGE adds its supplied condensed voice and a real FRAME rendering on white. The homepage imports neither work runtime; previews are static local media. The original three-panel composition and native-link behavior remain intact.

CHRONOS retains its supplied watch image, Cormorant Garamond typography and dark metal palette. Nine native entrances share three gallery rows on desktop and one sequence on mobile. Each panel retains its work's typography and palette.

INTERVAL retains its original threshold imagery and supplied Bodoni Moda voice in the second triptych. Its mobile panel keeps the established vertical sequence. FORM and FUSION complete a third triptych with captured media from their actual runtimes; the homepage still loads no work runtime.
