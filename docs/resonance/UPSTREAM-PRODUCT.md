# RESONANCE — Sound Made Visible

## Purpose
An immersive, browser-native audiovisual installation. One source of vibration evolves through silence, a plucked string, propagation, interference, a resonant plate, harmonics, recording, and playback. The visitor explores through native scrolling and deliberate acoustic interactions.

## Audience and platform
Curious visitors without an acoustics background; desktop at 1080p, 1440p and 4K, plus recomposed narrow mobile screens. Semantic HTML remains meaningful without graphics. Audio is opt-in and entirely synthesized locally.

## User commitments
The supplied RESONANCE brief fixes the eight-scene arc, graphite/ivory material language, restrained amber or cyan accents, two persistent systems (source and trace), continuous reversible handoffs, physically grounded motion, and a full production project with documentation. No music app, generic analyzer, static-image gallery, or disconnected sections.

## Success
Vibration becomes space; particles reveal nodal structure; harmonics become an etched signal that can be captured and played back. Each scene has an enter, readable hold, living hold, handoff and exit. Rapid scrubbing, reverse scrolling, resize, restore and reduced motion remain usable.

## Implementation assumptions
English is the exhibition language, matching the supplied copy. No microphone access, account, paid service or external API is needed. Technical visuals are explicitly qualitative and time-slowed where appropriate. The final deliverable is a clean, portable ZIP. The user explicitly prohibits deployment through OpenAI Sites; do not publish the website.
