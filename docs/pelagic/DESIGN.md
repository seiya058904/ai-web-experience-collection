# PELAGIC — A Language of Living Light

Five English observations move through a dark, research-inspired ocean: the difference between our light and an animal's, an encounter with a lured fish, patterned skin, and the collective signals of a colony. Preserve the near-black teal field, cream Cormorant Garamond and Manrope typography, the survey-light control, encounter replay and scrubbing, the observation chapters and the Canvas 2D renderer with its complete SVG edition.

Every organism is an authored, illustrative reconstruction; meanings of the patterns stay open. Supplied research, asset manifest, image-generation record and QA remain in `provenance/pelagic/upstream/`.

## Boundary of the collection frame

The Collection adds the independent document, the `/pelagic/` route, base-aware URLs and the shared return control at the head of the exhibition navigation. Observations, controls, copy and timings are unchanged.
