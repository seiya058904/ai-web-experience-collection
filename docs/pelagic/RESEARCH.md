# PELAGIC — research and representation

The exhibition is inspired by published research on deep-sea bioluminescence, siphonophore colonies, Humboldt squid skin and Erenna-like lures (MBARI, Monterey Bay Aquarium, PNAS, Dunn Lab). None of the artwork is a photograph of a specimen, a measured signal or a recorded hunt; the light field, animal geometry and encounter timing are authored. Prize-worthy claims are absent by design.

Cormorant Garamond and Manrope keep their OFL notices; GSAP and Lenis notices are in `public/pelagic/licenses/`. No overall upstream source license was declared.
