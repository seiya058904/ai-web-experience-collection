## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A complete immersive digital exhibition: Rome in Marble and Memory. The visitor experiences how sculpture embodies power, divinity, identity, death and survival. The attached brief is the authoritative product record.

## Stack
Implementation choice delegated by the brief's medium-by-medium principle. Use a self-contained static browser application, local assets, and deterministic WebGL where depth is essential. No backend is needed.

## Users
Visitors exploring a cinematic Roman sculpture exhibition on desktop or mobile. The brief requires 1080p, 1440p, 4K, and mobile recomposition.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Nine linked chapters: Colossus, Stone Becomes Flesh, Face of Power, Body/Authority, Monument, History Carved in Stone, Fracture, Afterlife, Aeterna. AI visual exploration before implementation. Real spatial fragment motion and relief depth. One native scroll architecture. Reverse, refresh, resize, reduced motion and offline local operation must work. Deliver a ZIP containing source, production assets, selected visual references, models, licenses and build instructions. No hosting, publishing, Sites capability, public previews or repository mutations.

## Brand Commitments
AETERNA — Rome in Marble and Memory. Monumental, sculptural, tactile, cinematic, precise. Marble, limestone, bronze, stone dust; ivory, chalk, charcoal. No black-and-gold template. The strongest medium for each image. Persistent body and fragment motifs.

## Evidence on Hand
The supplied full brief; the selected AI direction and scene decisions in design/VISUAL_BIBLE.md; museum research and object records in docs/CURATORIAL_NOTES.md and docs/MUSEUM_ASSETS.md; asset identities and licenses in docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md and docs/AI_ASSETS.json. Original generated works are labeled as imagined/interpretive, not catalogued historical objects. Local validation scope and its limits are recorded in docs/VERIFICATION.md.

## Product Principles
Visual quality precedes technical spectacle. Motion follows carving, revealing, assembling, fracturing and preserving. Every control performs a real action. Historic facts are sourced. The complete runnable deliverable remains local.
