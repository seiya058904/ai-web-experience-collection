# LUTHIER — Supporting Scene Copy

These are concise supporting explanations for the nine-scene experience. Final display headings and typographic breaks may differ. Chinese translations are reference text for readers of the project documentation; they do not imply that the interface includes a language switch.

Source keys refer to [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). Poetic lines are original writing. Scientific relationships follow the cited research; the visual experience is an artistic interpretation.

## 01 — Presence

**English:** Before the first note, there is an object: a curved body, four strings and a surface that gathers light. Stay with its silence.

**中文参考：** 第一声之前，先有一件物体：弧形琴身、四根琴弦，以及承接光线的表面。先停留在它的寂静里。

## 02 — Wood

**English:** Fine spruce grain. Rippling maple. Dark ebony. Each material brings its own surface, and the maker gives those differences a shared form.

**中文参考：** 细密的云杉纹理，起伏的枫木光泽，深沉的乌木。每种材料都有自己的表情，制琴者让它们共同成为一个形体。

**Research:** R1–R2.

## 03 — Craft

**English:** An arch is shaped, an edge refined, a joint fitted. The instrument takes form through many small decisions that remain visible in the wood.

**中文参考：** 拱面被雕出，边缘被修整，接合处被细细配准。无数微小的决定，让乐器逐渐成形，也在木头上留下痕迹。

**Research:** R7; the description is a concise craft interpretation, not a complete construction sequence.

## 04 — Inside

**English:** Between two curved plates, a slender sound post connects top and back. Beneath the top, the bass bar follows the other side of the bridge.

**中文参考：** 两片弧形琴板之间，一根纤细的音柱连接着面板与背板。面板内侧，低音梁沿琴桥另一侧纵向延伸。

**Research:** R12. In the illustration, sound post placement belongs to the E-string side; the bass bar belongs to the G-string side.

## 05 — Tension

**English:** Four strings held ready. The bridge stands beneath their pressure. The surface becomes still enough for the smallest coming movement to matter.

**中文参考：** 四根琴弦已经绷紧。琴桥立在弦的压力之下。画面逐渐安静，让即将发生的细微运动变得清晰。

**Research:** R6. Tension is preparation; sustained sound requires energy from playing.

## 06 — First Bow

**English:** Rosined hair meets the string. It grips, draws and releases, repeating within a single stroke. The bow keeps feeding the motion it has begun.

**中文参考：** 附着松香的弓毛触到琴弦。咬住、牵引、释放，在一次运弓中反复发生。弓持续为刚刚开始的振动注入能量。

**Research:** R4. The hair crosses the speaking string between fingerboard and bridge; the bow stick stays clear of the instrument. Motion is slowed and enlarged for visibility.

## 07 — Resonance

**English:** Motion passes through the bridge into the wooden body. Different regions respond together, shaping the sound that reaches the surrounding air.

**中文参考：** 运动经由琴桥传入木质琴身。不同区域共同回应，塑造着最终传入周围空气的声音。

**Research:** R3, R5. The animated contours are original interpretations, not measured vibration modes.

## 08 — Sound as Space

**English:** The object recedes. Its curves remain as a field of motion, opening into the space around it. A visible interpretation of something heard.

**中文参考：** 物体渐渐退后，曲线留下，成为向周围展开的运动场。这是对听觉的可视化想象。

**Interpretation:** The field is an artistic visualization rather than literal airborne lines or an acoustic measurement.

## 09 — Return

**English:** The motion settles. The wood returns to quiet. Nothing about its shape has changed; the silence now contains everything you have seen.

**中文参考：** 振动渐息，木头重归安静。它的形状并未改变，而此刻的寂静，已经包含你刚刚看见的一切。

## Shared closing line

**Shape the wood. Hold the tension. Let silence become sound.**

**中文参考：** 雕出木头的形状，留住弦上的张力，让寂静成为声音。
