# FERRUM — research and representation

FERRUM is an authored digital sculpture and a poetic six-act study of light, force and material. It does not reconstruct a named historical blade, prescribe a forging process, or represent measured steel behavior. “The Memory of Fire” and “The Relic” are artistic framing. Supplied research and image-generation records remain in `provenance/ferrum/upstream/`; treat them as source context, not as claims established by the rendered work.

Antonio, Barlow Condensed and Manrope retain their OFL notices. Three.js and Lenis retain their MIT notices in `public/ferrum/licenses/`. No project-wide upstream license was declared, so no overall source-license claim is made.
