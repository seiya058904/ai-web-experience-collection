# FERRUM — The Memory of Fire

Six English acts follow one original abstract steel sculpture from rough blank through heat, force, cooling and edge reveal to a quiet final display. Preserve its graphite, bone and ember palette, Antonio/Manrope typography, slow chapter handoffs, optional sound, inspection controls and supplied still edition. “Relic” is poetic chapter language: the work does not claim historical identity, provenance, or a complete forging process.

The detailed visual bible, source research and design references are preserved in `provenance/ferrum/upstream/`. Runtime stills are supplied artwork; the interactive steel material is authored geometry and shading.
