# KAGE / VOID

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

The user delegates the implementation choice to the approach that best serves visual impact and smoothness. The project will use TypeScript, Vite, Three.js and one manually driven Lenis instance.

## Product Purpose

A continuous, scroll-driven, black-and-white spatial art installation. The sculpture, its transformations, and the visitor's movement through it are the content.

## Operating Context

Desktop at 1080p, 1440p and 4K, plus a separately composed mobile experience. Mouse wheel, trackpad, touch and keyboard navigation must remain usable. The deliverable is a complete, reproducible ZIP; no site registration, deployment or hosted preview is authorized.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One recurring system of lines, apertures, planes and masses evolves through VOID, LINE, FRAME, LIGHT, MASS, SHADOW, FORM and SILENCE. Enter, impact, composition, a living hold and handoff should read as a single continuous movement. Reverse scrolling, resizing and tab restoration must preserve the scene.

## Brand Commitments

Pure black, white and neutral grey; strong contrast, spatial scale, shadow, minimal copy, Japanese spatial restraint. No tourism, culture or architecture information site. No conventional feature-card sections.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor prefers-reduced-motion, offer a visible motion control, keep navigation keyboard accessible, preserve an intelligible static experience when WebGL is unavailable, and avoid mandatory sound or autoplay audio.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's detailed creative and technical brief. All artwork is original procedural geometry; third-party code and typeface licenses must accompany the deliverable.

## Product Principles

- The visual transformation is the narrative.
- Preserve one spatial identity across the entire scroll.
- A stopped frame should form a deliberate composition.
- Ship the complete project and necessary resources without caches or temporary QA output.
