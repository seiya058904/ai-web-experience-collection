# DOSE — The chosen tablet and process

DOSE follows one **hypothetical, uncoated, immediate-release, round tablet**. Its visual form is unscored and shallowly convex on both faces. The artwork explores how prepared materials can become a compact and later change on contact with liquid.

The narrative uses **direct compression**. It begins with prepared ingredients, passes through blending, die filling and compaction, and then changes to observation of the finished form. Wet granulation, dry granulation, drying and film coating are not stages in this selected branch. Other products can use different routes; the choice here is a coherent subject for the artwork, not a recommendation for manufacture. The route research is documented in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md#3-ishikawa-et-al--direct-compression-and-granulation).

## What the artwork abstracts

### Materials and distribution

The active ingredient is deliberately unnamed, with no molecule, amount or therapeutic claim assigned to it. The excipient labels group visual roles and are not an ingredient list. A material described as a “matrix” is the surrounding formulation material in this visual model, not a claim of a controlled-release tablet.

The colored grain counts, relative grain sizes and internal arrangement are artistic choices. Particle paths are authored trajectories, not a validated granular-mechanics model or laboratory mixing experiment. Their apparent balance does not prove either blend homogeneity or content uniformity. [FDA's qualitative background](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/inspection-guides/oral-solid-dosage-forms-prepost-approval-issues-194) explains why material differences and handling matter.

### Compaction and surface

The die, punches and tablet form a simplified spatial explanation. The animation is not an operating sequence for any identified machine. Changes in particle shape, contact, void space and density are condensed into the movement of the powder bed and appearance of the compact; their magnitudes are not measured.

Pores and surface relief are generated material cues. They do not map the pore network of a real tablet. There is no added coating stage, membrane, or shell. The same uncoated object identity continues through the surface and internal views.

### Observation and quality

The Evidence chapter is **original conceptual CGI, not a CT scan**. The depth control changes the drawn section and does not collect data, measure composition, identify ingredients, inspect purity, or certify quality.

Content uniformity concerns active-ingredient content among individual dosage units. A view inside one tablet cannot establish that result. The distinction between visual observation and quality testing follows [FDA / ICH Q6A](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/q6a-specifications-test-procedures-and-acceptance-criteria-new-drug-substances-and-new-drug-products). No analytical results, acceptance badge or regulatory status is attached to the hypothetical tablet.

### Contact with liquid

**Disintegration is not dissolution.** Breakup can leave undissolved particles; dissolution concerns soluble material entering solution. The artwork uses fragments and fine material to introduce the distinction. It does not claim that each visible object is a molecule, or that all ingredients dissolve together. The [USP concept](https://www.usp.org/sites/default/files/usp/document/harmonization/gen-chapter/april-2019-m99460.pdf) and [FDA dissolution background](https://www.fda.gov/media/70936/download) support this explanation; source-access limits are recorded in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

The liquid is a visual setting, not an identified physiological or test medium. Fragment shape, amount, motion, opacity and pacing are illustrative. “Immediate release” does not mean instantaneous disappearance. No release rate, absorption, drug effect or patient outcome can be inferred from the sequence.

## Reversible navigation

Scroll position controls the presented scene. Scrolling backward revisits an earlier illustration; it does **not** assert that a pressed tablet physically returns to loose powder, that dissolved material spontaneously reforms, or that a real manufacturing process can be reversed.

The final circular composition echoes the opening as an editorial device. **Return to the beginning** is navigation. The viewing controls change presentation, not the scientific properties of a tablet.
