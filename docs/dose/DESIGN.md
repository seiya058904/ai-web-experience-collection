# DOSE — Visual Bible

## Direction

Calibrated still life: a precise industrial catalogue becomes an inhabited material study. The visitor remains with one unscored, uncoated, round biconvex tablet as scale changes. The surface mode is **Experience**. Artwork leads; controls stay quiet.

The supplied brief fixes material, palette, scope and narrative. Initial concepts compared a porcelain studio with a dark photographic observatory. The porcelain direction was selected for surface legibility and continuity with powder and steel. Dark green is reserved for the Evidence chapter. The serif dark opening was rejected because it shifted the work toward luxury branding and reduced the legibility of pores. Concept images are art-direction references, not production backgrounds or scientific image data.

## Allowed opening copy

DOSE; Index; About; The architecture of a tablet.; The smallest objects can hold the most deliberate design.; Enter the matter; 00 / PROLOGUE; Scroll to explore; Conceptual CGI · Not to scale.

The final compact CGI/scale label replaces the concept's nonessential “A study in material & scale” footer to keep the scientific status visible throughout. No measurements, fake specimen identifiers, performance claims or marketing badges are added.

All chapters provide Enter → Stable composition → Living hold → Handoff → Exit. Compaction and material history are deterministic functions of scroll; reverse scroll revisits the visualization, not a claim of physically reversible manufacturing. Typography occupies a safe left zone on desktop. On mobile it moves above the centered artwork with short supporting text below. The final section has enough travel to stop on a completed composition.

## Rendering and interaction contract

The primary art is original procedural Three.js geometry, local material textures generated by code, and instanced particles. A single application-owned animation clock updates Lenis, scene state and rendering. No GSAP ticker or second continuous rendering loop exists; occasional one-shot Lenis scroll-event callbacks are distinct from the central render clock. The global scroll position is the source of truth. There is no mandatory scroll snapping or locked wheel navigation.

Observe depth is a labeled native range with keyboard support and qualitative endpoint labels, not a measured scan value. Index and About use native dialogs with visible close controls, focus restoration and Escape support. Every narrative chapter remains semantic HTML. Reduced motion removes continuous movement, uses native scrolling, and shortens chapter spacing. The non-WebGL edition preserves the full story and geometry-led static diagrams with a 2D canvas; no empty viewport is an acceptable fallback.

## Concept provenance

`docs/concepts/` contains the selected generated references. Their exact prompts are in the adjacent concept records. No reference photograph or paper figure is copied into the application. The shipped visual scene is original code, so a viewer can reverse the process and adjust observation depth.

## Intermediate backgrounds

A viewer can stop anywhere in a dark-to-light handoff. The application checks linear-sRGB contrast for all three reading colors at each intermediate background and temporarily uses black or white when the stable palette would fall below 4.5:1. Stable chapter colors remain unchanged. The regression test samples 1,001 positions per chapter for ink, secondary text and accent.

## Collection adaptation (12 October 2026)

Runtime source is in `experiences/dose/src/`, its independent document is `/dose/`, and local resources/notices are in `public/dose/`. The Collection reuses its installed Three.js 0.186.1 and Lenis 1.3.26. Standalone vendor modules, duplicate dist and startup/build tooling are omitted; generated concept references remain in scoped provenance. Chapter spans are 2.5 / 2.8 / 3.05 / 3.6 / 2.65 / 3.35 / 2.7 viewport heights. Original local process timing and the 0.78 handoff start are retained; the first six handoffs have approximately 39–40% more scroll travel. Caption fade ranges avoid concurrent readable captions. Desktop endpoint headings and supporting copy share a flex frame with a protected gap, and type sizes respond to viewport height as well as width. See ACCEPTANCE.md for actual checks and limits.

The Collection handoff aligns both material representations in one continuous world frame (position, quaternion and scale), fades press machinery ahead of the tablet, and interpolates key/rim lighting at every boundary. No independent clock, geometry replacement or simulated physical reversibility is introduced.
