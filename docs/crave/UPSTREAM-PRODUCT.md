# CRAVE — Before the First Bite

## Platform
Web, local development and offline-capable local preview. Desktop, 4K and mobile browsers.

## Purpose
An immersive, visual, food-material experience about appetite before eating. Food must look edible and desirable. The audience observes heat, browning, crust, moisture, viscosity, reflection, steam and the anticipation of a first bite.

## Stack
Implementation choice delegated by the comprehensive brief: choose media for the shot. Vanilla JavaScript and Vite are the intended lightweight foundation; Lenis is the sole global smooth-scroll authority, integrated with GSAP and ScrollTrigger on one clock. Self-host all runtime dependencies, production media and fonts. No service credentials.

## Binding user requirements
- Eleven scenes: Desire, Heat, Brown, Crust, Inside, Melt, Glaze, Steam, Plate, Cut, Before the First Bite.
- Research, generated visual studies, three Hero candidates, comparison, refinement and appetite curation precede code.
- Four to six representative food objects/materials. Food is material; the site is neither a menu, recipe, shop nor catalog.
- Material match cuts and shared specular light connect the scenes.
- Each core scene includes an authored visual system. Motion alternates hold, anticipation, snap and release.
- Restrained text. Extreme crops, natural colors, credible texture and moisture. No people or restaurant setting.
- Mobile has deliberate vertical compositions. Honor reduced motion and stop expensive work when hidden.
- Real browser verification at scene holds, reverse/fast/slow scrolling, mobile, 4K, resize and tab restore.
- No deployment, Sites, public preview or hosting integration.
- Final response and deliverable: ZIP only, containing source, selected assets, fonts, build configuration, README, attribution, licenses and provenance. Exclude dependencies, caches, traces, rejected generations, QA screenshots and duplicated builds.

## Decisions delegated within the brief
The AI selects the strongest Hero and specific representative foods after comparison. Sound is optional and never starts without a user action. English is the exhibition language as established by the supplied title and scene vocabulary; Chinese setup instructions aid the owner.
