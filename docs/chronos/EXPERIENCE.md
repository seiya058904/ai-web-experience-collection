## Direction contract

**THESIS.** Bring the visitor inside a timepiece. Follow the brief's Time Machine direction; avoid shopping layouts, card grids and generic section templates.

**OWN-WORLD.** Preserve Dark / Luxury / Precise / Minimal / Cinematic: graphite space, silver surfaces, restrained champagne accents, light serif narrative type and small ruled controls.

**STORY.** Move from macro object to micro mechanism and back to the object. Six scenes carry that perception: Time, Energy, Transmission, Oscillation, Craft, Chronos. The visitor scrolls forward, reverses, pauses and inspects the movement.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Place the oversized watch on the right, the opening sentence and entry action on the left, the small wordmark and index above, and chapter/transport controls below. Compact and shallow landscape layouts preserve reading space.

**FORM.** Use one persistent sticky canvas with layered HTML copy. Camera position, assembly transforms and mechanical phase carry between scenes; stable compositions precede handoffs. This form is explicitly pinned by the brief; no sampled form rank or random seed is asserted.
