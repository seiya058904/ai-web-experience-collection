# CHRONOS

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

An original immersive web artwork that lets the visitor travel into a mechanical watch by scrolling. The primary experience is continuous, reversible visual storytelling. It is not a shop or an encyclopedia.

## Users

Visitors exploring a visual showcase on desktop or mobile. The brief prioritizes 1080p, 1440p, 4K and phone viewports.

## Stack

Implementation choice within the user's stated techniques: Vite, TypeScript, Three.js, GSAP/ScrollTrigger, Lenis, semantic HTML and CSS. Static application; no backend or account required.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scroll drives the camera and mechanical assembly; the same mechanism carries between scenes.
- Each scene has enter, stable composition, living hold, handoff and exit.
- Mechanically credible direction, ratios and oscillator/escapement relationships.
- All assets self-contained. ZIP delivery with source, dependencies manifest, build output, instructions and attribution.
- No deployment, hosting, remote publishing or repository mutation.
- Reduced motion, keyboard access and responsive compositions.

## Brand Commitments

CHRONOS — The Architecture of Time. Dark, luxurious, precise, cinematic, minimal; silver, graphite and sparing champagne metal. Original art and geometry; no claims of affiliation with a watch manufacturer.

## Evidence on Hand

User's complete creative and technical brief. Six generated scene studies and two original production artworks. Primary mechanical references are documented in ATTRIBUTION.md.

## Product Principles

1. Show a complete composition before handing it off.
2. Keep the world continuous in both scroll directions.
3. Motion earns its place through mechanical or spatial meaning.
4. Honest cinematic model, no invented manufacturing or accuracy claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable complete copy, reduced motion, visible keyboard focus, functional index and no audio autoplay.
