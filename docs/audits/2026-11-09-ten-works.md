# Ten supplied works — import and content audit, 9 November 2026

IMAGO, SILLAGE, AER, BERTH, MEND, FRAME, ODDS, RALLY, SENTE and PAS, imported from ten ZIPs whose byte lengths and SHA-256 matched the approved inventory before extraction. Per-work results are in `docs/<route>/ACCEPTANCE.md`; every ZIP entry is listed in `provenance/deliveries/import-manifest.json`.

## Delivery boundary

- No path traversal, absolute paths, nested archives or executables in any ZIP. Each was extracted to its own empty directory outside the repository.
- Imported: runtime source, runtime media and fonts, licence and notice texts, and supplied text records (design, requirements, sources, verification history, prompts and generation records, tests) under `provenance/<route>/upstream/`.
- Not committed and recorded as omitted: compiled `dist/`, package and lock files, independent Vite/TypeScript configs, start and serve scripts, offline/portable/QA pages, design-tool caches, checksum lists, build and test logs, verification evidence, generated masters and concepts, reference images, and PAS's MakeHuman model-source binaries (its licence and metadata texts are kept).
- PAS keeps its full runtime frame set: 4,699 WebP frames plus manifest (about 85 MB) under `public/pas/frames/`. The largest single deployed file is a 5.1 MB model buffer.
- All imported files are byte-identical to their ZIP entries except 30 integration-edited source files, each named in the work's acceptance record.

## Dependencies and fonts

- Root versions are used: Lenis 1.3.26 (supplied 1.3.27), three 0.186.1 (supplied 0.187.0, AER/MEND/PAS), TypeScript 5.9.3 (supplied 7.0.2 or 5.9.3). No dependency or lockfile changed. The only type fix is ODDS's `String.prototype.toWellFormed` declaration.
- None of the supplied `@fontsource*` packages is installed. Fonts are declared locally. Binaries byte-identical to existing Collection files reuse those URLs (Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans, Manrope, Bodoni Moda, Barlow Condensed); Cormorant Garamond 500, shared by BERTH/IMAGO/SILLAGE, is added once under `public/shared/fonts/`. New font binaries: Inter (IMAGO), Newsreader and DM Sans variable (MEND), four Manrope subsets (ODDS), Barlow Condensed 600 italic and DM Sans 600 (RALLY).

## Content review

- **Brands and real people:** no logos or brand imagery. SILLAGE cites one Fragonard product page as labelled context ("the product and its ingredients are not reproduced"); it stays. Characters (BERTH, PAS, RALLY, MEND) are invented; PAS's dancers are rendered from CC0 MakeHuman assets.
- **Claims:** IMAGO calls its images artistic reconstructions; AER states its landscape, wind and routes are fiction and not flying instruction; SILLAGE states it describes no perfume formula; ODDS calls its model ideal and its animation non-physical; RALLY calls its trajectories a deterministic educational model. No health, medical or performance claims were found.
- **Licences:** every shipped font and library has its notice in `public/<route>/licenses/`. ODDS shipped none; the standard Manrope OFL and Lenis MIT texts are copied from existing Collection notices. IMAGO, RALLY and PAS declare MIT for their own source; AER's project notice leaves publication to the owner; the other six declare no application licence.
- **Links:** external links are citations (FAA, ITF, NIST, Penn State, NIH/NIDCD, US FWS, AMNH, Smithsonian Gardens, Monarch Joint Venture, Monarch Watch, British Go Association, University of Sydney, Fragonard). Every `target="_blank"` link carries `rel="noreferrer"` or `noopener`. Links were not fetched, so liveness is unverified.
- **Images and language:** content images carry alt text in the supplied markup; decorative layers use empty alt. All ten are English.
- **Runtime:** no `localhost`, `127.0.0.1` or `terminal.local` reference remains. In the browser sessions recorded in the acceptance files, every request stayed on the site origin and no console errors appeared. AER's two SVG path errors were fixed by adding the missing number separators.
