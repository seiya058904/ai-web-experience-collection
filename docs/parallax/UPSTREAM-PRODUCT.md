# PARALLAX — Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Implementation choice within the user's explicitly open medium strategy: vanilla DOM/CSS, bundled Three.js and custom shaders, local image assets, a dependency-free Node build script, and a dependency-free Node localhost server. No runtime CDN, backend, or deployment integration.

## Users
Visitors exploring an immersive digital art installation on desktop or mobile. Desktop compositions must support 1080p, 1440p and 4K. The brief requires a composed mobile experience and reduced motion.

## Product Purpose
Exhibit one original sculpture, THE IMPOSSIBLE FORM, through ten perceptual states: OBJECT, FRAGMENT, ALIGNMENT, REFLECTION, RIFT, MEMBRANE, CHROMA, HALO, REASSEMBLY, THE MUSEUM.

## Positioning
A form does not exist only in matter. It also exists in the act of seeing. The visitor reconstructs one object through perspective, fragmentation, reflection, optical surfaces and light.

## Capabilities and Constraints
ImageGen art direction precedes implementation. Generate and curate multiple hero options, material studies and a target keyframe for every major scene. Use AI assets directly where suitable. Actual camera alignment, moving fragments, dynamic reflection, rift depth and reassembly use genuine geometry/rendering. One native document scroll authority, reversible states, resize, refresh, tab restore and accessible navigation.

## Brand Commitments
PARALLAX — The Museum of Impossible Forms. Avant-garde, sculptural, luxurious, editorial, precise, spatial and uncanny. Original angular architectural form, not a sphere, metaball, blob or twisted torus. Quiet deliberate motion; no particles or arbitrary continuous rotation.

## Delivery
Only one ZIP containing complete source, all production assets and used generated images, model and texture data, selected Visual Bible references, build configuration, README and provenance. Exclude node_modules, caches, rejected generations, drafts, screenshots, traces and debug files. No public hosting, preview URL, repository publication, or OpenAI Sites calls. Localhost build and browser QA are authorized.

## Product Principles
- Design the image before choosing its medium.
- Keep one recognizable sculpture across every scene.
- Choose the strongest image and implement every promised spatial behavior honestly.
- Let artwork lead from the first frame, with no intro gate.
- Keep the local deliverable complete and self-contained.
