# RELAY — A Message Finds Its Way

Six chapters follow one deterministic, browser-local delivery. A sentence is encoded to UTF-8, split into five-byte blocks, routed through two channels and reassembled in original order. Preserve its warm paper (#F1EBDD), ink and vermilion palette, Archivo and IBM Plex Mono typography, the native SVG sculpture, the delivery controls, the recorded event strip and the honest endings: **Received** and **Confirmation pending** may disagree, and an ended round can read **Incomplete**.

The work is a demonstration protocol, not TCP, QUIC or BGP. It carries no network traffic, stores no message and promises no successful delivery. The supplied model specification, engine review, visual bible, visual review and known limitations stay in `provenance/relay/upstream/docs/`; treat them as source context.

## Boundary of the collection frame

The Collection adds only an independent document, an `/relay/` route, base-aware asset URLs and the shared return control in the work's own header navigation. Chapter names, controls, seeded scenarios, timings and copy are unchanged.
