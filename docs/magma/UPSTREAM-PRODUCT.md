# MAGMA — Stone Before Stone

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Implementation choice delegated by the request to design and implement the entire work: portable static HTML, CSS and native JavaScript modules; Lenis for smooth scrolling; one custom WebGL material compositor. All dependencies and media are local in the final package. No backend.

## Product Purpose
An immersive digital sculpture about luminous molten material becoming dark, still stone. The visitor follows Heat → Flow → Skin → Crack → Cool → Glass → Stone through nine material scenes.

## Users
Visitors viewing a flagship visual web artwork on a desktop or mobile browser. Success is a felt continuity of material, weight and cooling, as explicitly requested.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Nine scenes: Pressure, Molten, Flow, Skin, Crack, Glass, Fracture, Crystal / Stone, Sealed.
- Fissure and crust are shared material motifs; no conventional product disassembly.
- Six authored visual systems: molten material, crust growth, fissure emission, cooling state, obsidian material, fracture geometry.
- Look development precedes implementation, including generated comparisons and curation.
- Lenis is the only smooth-scroll authority. Native touch scrolling remains available.
- Mobile retains the whole story with bounded rendering cost; reduced motion and no-WebGL fallback are supported.
- ZIP only. No public or private hosting, no deployment, no OpenAI Sites.
- Retain source, selected assets, shaders, build instructions and provenance. Exclude dependencies, caches, debug files, test screenshots, failed renders and duplicate builds.

## Brand Commitments
Darkness holding internal fire. Dominant volcanic / obsidian black; charcoal, ash, ember orange, molten red and very limited near-white heat. Heavy, controlled camera and material motion. No disaster imagery, fantasy lava, generic neon, excessive particles or floaty camera orbit.

## Evidence on Hand
The user supplied the full narrative and aesthetic brief. Authoritative geological research informs material behavior. Visual assets are AI-generated artistic material studies, not documentary photography or a numerical thermodynamics simulation.

## Product Principles
1. The work itself leads; controls recede.
2. Images define the material quality; code authors the transformation.
3. Continuity and weight outrank ornament.
4. Scientific distinction matters: obsidian and basalt are different compositions / cooling histories, not inevitable sequential stages of the same specimen.
