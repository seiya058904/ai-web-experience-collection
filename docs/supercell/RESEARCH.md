# SUPERCELL — research and representation

The storm is a single imagined supercell, illustrated rather than reconstructed. NOAA/NSSL and NASA explainer pages and Berndnaut Smilde's cloud work informed the visual language only; no photograph, dataset or code was imported. Section densities, shear response, contour lines and parcel paths are authored explanatory geometry, not measured meteorology.

Manrope keeps its OFL notice; GSAP and Lenis notices are in `public/supercell/licenses/`. No overall upstream source license was declared.
