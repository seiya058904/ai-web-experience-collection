# SUPERCELL — The Shape of Moving Air

Five English chapters observe one **illustrated** storm: its weight, the airflow that sustains it, a movable section plane, a change of wind shear, and a quiet closing. Preserve the deep storm blue, bone and amber palette, Manrope typography, the section controls (slice height, reference/change shear, find the updraft), the optional local sound and the Canvas 2D rendering path with its still poster.

The storm is an illustration, not a forecast or an observed event. The supplied visual bible, research, reviews and source/license record stay in `provenance/supercell/upstream/`.

## Boundary of the collection frame

Only the independent document, the `/supercell/` route, base-aware asset URLs and the shared return control in the header are new. Chapter copy, projection geometry, handoffs and controls are unchanged.
