# NIB — Final Visual Review

Review date: **2026-10-07**. The finished local build was reviewed against the curated Visual Bible, actual desktop and mobile captures, intermediate material handoffs and sampled implementation source. The reviewer worked independently of the implementation pass.

## First review

The initial disposition was **fix**. The review accepted the monumental desktop opening, type hierarchy, graphite-to-ivory ground, discreet navigation, independent mobile composition, stationary paper plane, shared stroke and sparse final trace. It identified three material joins that required more work:

1. The mobile opening needed an optically distinct attached bead below the tipping.
2. The Meniscus-to-Contact turn flattened a raster image into an implausibly thin strip.
3. The macro line needed to occupy the cellulose structure rather than sit above it as a continuous ribbon.

## Corrective work

The mobile opening received its own transparent portrait material, with a small attached blue-black bead. The camera handoff now travels through the held liquid interface and returns to a real oblique nib view with visible metal curvature and feed thickness. That same pose continues through contact and writing. Its lacquer extends beyond the source crop using sampled photographic boundaries.

Absorption now samples the paper material. Dye follows small interstitial paths; localized staining remains close to the stroke; raised photographic fibers partially occlude the wet body. The centerline, contact progress, camera geometry and drying trajectory remain shared.

## Verdict pass

The reviewer reopened the regenerated desktop and mobile matrices, the three corrected material views, the liquid-to-paper handoff frames, and Contact and Write views. The scope was the three scored findings plus directly introduced regressions.

The returned findings were:

- **Resolved — mobile held meniscus.** The small attached bead has a readable lower boundary and reflection at the 390-pixel composition.
- **Resolved — Meniscus → Contact material handoff.** The view crosses the optical interface and reveals one oblique nib; the hairline collapse is absent.
- **Resolved — ink within the cellulose structure.** Fiber crests interrupt the ink body and nearby staining stays confined to one controlled stroke.
- **Remaining: Clear.** The sampled Contact and Write frames retain dimensional metal, a tip that meets the stroke, continuous lacquer beyond the crop and separate copy space.

The final disposition, preserved verbatim:

> disposition: ship

## Scope and limits

This verdict covers the scored fixes; it is not a certification of every possible viewport or device. It was based on actual captures and sampled source, not an independently operated physical device. The separate [QA record](QA.md) documents the 56 browser assertions, lifecycle regressions, local build checks and untested environments. No award, external showcase acceptance or measured physical simulation is claimed.

The external quality-bar catalog was unavailable in the execution environment. The design contract and available references were used without inventing an approved pixel comp or a missing comparison report. Temporary QA captures, browser traces and discarded image candidates are excluded from the delivery ZIP; the final source, all production assets and the curated Visual Bible remain reproducible locally.
