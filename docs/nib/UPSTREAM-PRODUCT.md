# NIB — Ink Under Pressure

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Implementation medium is delegated by the explicit creative-autonomy and medium-strategy brief. Use a locally runnable, portable static frontend with vendored Lenis and GSAP, one animation clock, Canvas/SVG authored material systems, and curated AI photographic assets. No backend or external runtime services.

## Users

Visitors exploring an immersive digital artwork about the fluid mechanism of a fountain pen. Desktop and mobile compositions are both required.

## Product Purpose

Follow a drop from reservoir through feed, slit, meniscus, paper contact and fibers until it becomes one controlled line. The visitor should experience a tiny fluid machine rather than a product catalog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ink out, replacement air in, pressure and capillarity together govern delivery.
- One Ink Line provides visual continuity across every scene.
- The key material transition is metal → liquid ink → ivory paper.
- Every major scene has authored live content and a stable, complete resting composition.
- Exactly one Lenis smooth-scroll authority and one animation clock; reversible progress, resizing, restore and reduced-motion support.
- No deployment, public hosting, Sites, GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages.
- Final delivery is one ZIP containing source, production assets, final AI assets, build instructions, licenses, attribution and provenance. No dependencies/cache/debug/traces/failed assets in that ZIP.

## Brand Commitments

NIB — Ink Under Pressure. Precise, liquid, tactile, intimate, metallurgical, editorial, luxurious, quiet and cinematic. Gold belongs to real metal highlights. Ink is blue-black; paper is warm ivory. Avoid generic black-gold commerce, explosion diagrams, particles, neon interfaces and calligraphy shows.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's complete creative brief. Original AI art was generated, compared and refined before implementation; the curated references are recorded in `docs/VISUAL-BIBLE.md`. Primary manufacturer and scientific references ground mechanism copy in `docs/RESEARCH.md` and `docs/MECHANISM.md`. The visual model is a qualitative interpretation, not an engineering simulation. Final material provenance, retained prompts and file digests are recorded in `docs/PROVENANCE.json` for the 2026-10-07 session.

## Product Principles

1. Hold the ink. Balance the air. Draw the line.
2. The material is the interface; controls recede.
3. Contact changes the world, from reflected light to absorbed ink.
4. Every quiet pause is an authored frame, including mobile and reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic readable narrative, keyboard-accessible chapter navigation, visible focus, no automatic audio, readable text safety zones, reduced-motion and low-cost rendering paths.
