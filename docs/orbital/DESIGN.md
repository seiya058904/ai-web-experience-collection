# ORBITAL — implemented direction

ORBITAL keeps its original condensed white typography, orange/ice engineering accents, Earth photography, shared launch vehicle, lunar globe, eight-scene mission and silent-by-default sound. It remains an illustrative journey, not a flight, structural or guidance simulation. Source/fact caveats and the reading companion remain available in Mission Index and Sources & credits.

## Navigation hierarchy

The bottom timeline is the one persistent chapter progress/navigation surface on desktop. On phones, that same role becomes a compact current-chapter/Index control between 44px Previous/Next buttons. The complete eight-chapter plan is on demand in Mission Index. The former top chapter shortcuts and duplicate right rail are removed; Collection, sound and Index retain their distinct header roles. A current chapter caption is status rather than a second chapter track.

## Machine study

Whole vehicle is the initial and reset view. Four local buttons select Propulsion, Structure, Guidance and Payload. The original descriptive copy remains in the whole/system explanations. Their grid shares its intrinsic maximum height, so selecting a system does not move the object simply because the prose length changes. Only the active explanation is exposed to assistive technology.

Each choice traces a specific illustrative role on the existing separated GLB: propellant/exhaust toward and below the engine group; parallel structural paths along booster and upper-stage shells; an orientation arc and correction connection at the conceptual guidance/interstage region; a protective envelope and schematic protected volume at the fairing. These authored paths describe a function, not calculated stress, flow, avionics location, engine actuation or payload geometry. The interface says **Diagrammatic paths · not a simulation**.

`study.ts` owns the common model-coordinate paths. WebGL and Canvas draw those same paths and select the corresponding supplied parts. Their offsets follow the original axial separation, including the booster/engine lateral shift. The paths appear only with the Machine chapter and after the corresponding model assets are available; Whole vehicle and other chapters remove them. No independent loop, camera or time easing is introduced. Reduced motion retains the same selectable causal diagrams without ambient advance.

## Reading space

The phone Moon is positioned and sized inside measured free space below the destination copy and above the compact progress control. WebGL converts that screen-space region through its existing camera; Canvas uses the same region directly. The globe remains visible without placing an opaque reading panel over it. A short phone therefore receives a smaller complete lunar disc; larger phones preserve a larger cropped globe. Short desktop destination typography/spacing clears header and progress captions.

The phone Machine view similarly fits its existing assembly into the available region below the study controls. Copy ResizeObserver callbacks mark geometry dirty; the existing GSAP ticker reads the measured layout only when needed and paints it with the original scene coordinates. Visibility suspension, DPR/pixel caps, one Lenis instance and its existing single clock remain.

Known chapter fragments, saved history and reading-companion offsets keep the precedence documented in [DESTINATION-AUDIT.md](DESTINATION-AUDIT.md) and [RESTORATION.md](RESTORATION.md). Current scoped evidence and limitations are in [BATCH-B-ACCEPTANCE.md](BATCH-B-ACCEPTANCE.md).
