# OPTIC — The Architecture of an Image

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the brief: freely choose a hybrid renderer after visual and scene design. Local-only source deliverable, with package/build configuration and no publication. Implementation choice: static HTML/CSS and JavaScript modules with Three.js, Vite build tool, no backend or runtime network requirements.

## Users

Visitors exploring a scroll-controlled photographic machine on 1080p, 1440p, 4K desktop and mobile browsers.

## Product Purpose

Follow one beam of light through an original mirrorless camera, from the outside world to a final photograph.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Nine continuous narrative scenes: light, optics, focus, aperture, shutter, sensor, stability, machine, image. Original credible full-frame camera architecture, original geometry, authored transitions, concise English typography. Keyboard navigation and reduced-motion mode. Deliver a clean independently runnable ZIP. Do not deploy, register a hosted site, or publish.

## Brand Commitments

OPTIC. Premium, optical, mechanical, tactile, cinematic, quiet and precise. No camera-brand product clone, commercial template, neon HUD, repetitive card reveals or image-only sections.

## Evidence on Hand

User's complete direction, narrative, engineering requirements and acceptance matrix. Primary-source research is collected in docs/SOURCES.md. The camera and specifications are an illustrative original design, not a real product.

## Product Principles

- The light carries the story; the machine explains it by moving.
- Mechanical and optical relationships remain internally consistent.
- Every scene has a stable composition and an object-led handoff.
- Photography is the quiet destination.
