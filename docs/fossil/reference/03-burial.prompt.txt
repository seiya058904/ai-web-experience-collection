Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: Full desktop scene concept for FOSSIL — Deep Time in Stone. One finished high-fidelity browserless website scene, 1600 × 1000 landscape, edge to edge. This is scene 03, BURIAL.
Primary request: Create an exceptional museum × geology × sculpture editorial web-design composition. The entire screen becomes a highly tactile cross section through layered dark shale and limestone. A single large ribbed planispiral ammonite is almost buried within the right-central lower layers: its faint bone-ivory contour, partial radial ribs and slight relief are still legible, while thin sediment laminae deliberately close over it. No living creature. The layers feel physically deposited, compressed and irregular, never like flat UI stripes.
Composition: Upper-left typography has generous dark negative space; the geological material occupies the entire frame and becomes more dimensional toward the lower right. The ammonite reads as a beautiful faint trace in stone, not a glowing symbol. Extremely subtle hairline registration marks and fine stratum captions at bottom left connect geology to exhibition graphics. Natural branching seams, mineral grain and a few irregular discontinuities. The screen feels like entering stone.
Lighting and palette: warm museum near-black #141512, bone ivory #e9e3d6, limestone #d9d0bd, shale and muted umber. Thin warm grazing light from above reveals small laminae. Dark but readable, soft light, deep sculptural shadow. No orange glow and no blue light.
Typography and exact text: Minimal top navigation, small widely spaced sans serif “FOSSIL” at top left, “INDEX +” at top right. Top bar has no container or filled button. Upper-left small caption “03 / BURIAL”. Below, exquisite large thin editorial serif headline, roman line “The surface” then an elegant italic line “closes.”. Below this, small disciplined sans serif copy exactly “Layer after layer, a form passes out of sight.”. Bottom-left tiny captions may be “STRATUM / 003” and “DEPOSITION”. All letters clean and intentional, restrained spacious hierarchy.
Visual design: museum catalog precision; the burial is composited with layered masks and specimen contour design. One strong scene, not a collage or a photograph with a caption. A stable moment within a patient scrolling installation.
Avoid: dinosaurs, prehistoric landscape, monsters, adventure, fantasy, medical HUD, cards, pills, dashboards, bright particles, gradients as a substitute for stone, generic black-orange sci-fi, contact sheets, device frames, watermarks. No extra copy.
