# BACKSTAGE — research and representation

The miniature theatre, its scenery, cues and posters are original. Supplied research and generation records document how the artwork was imagined; they do not identify a real venue, a historical production or a measured lighting rig. The acceptance cases describe tested interaction, not a claim about physical theatre practice.

Fraunces and Source Sans 3 keep their OFL notices; Three.js and Lenis notices are in `public/backstage/licenses/`. No overall upstream source license was declared.
