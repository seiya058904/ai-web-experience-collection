# BACKSTAGE — The Art of Making Believe

Five acts walk a visitor from a cream-framed miniature theatre through its working side, a scenery change, the director's controls and the ending you caused. Preserve the paper, ink-green, lacquer-red and lantern palette, Fraunces and Source Sans 3 typography, the Three.js stage, the supplied posters, the still mode and the complete static edition (including `?static=1`).

The theatre is an original working model, not a reproduction of a specific venue or production. The visual bible, art review, geometry check, state record, generation records and acceptance cases stay in `provenance/backstage/upstream/`.
