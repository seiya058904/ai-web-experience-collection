# MURMURATION — No one is leading.

Five chapters observe collective motion: the many, a local world with three rules, a turn that travels, a live field you can change, and the shape you leave. Preserve the warm grey sky, ink and ochre palette, Anton, Cormorant and DM Sans typography, the recorded flight, the live rules and presets, Hold/Step, the still mode and the fieldprint export.

There is no leader in the model and no scripted formation. Bird scale, colour and camera choices are artistic; the recording is a reversible sample of an actual simulation. Research notes, architecture, QA and image-generation records stay in `provenance/murmuration/upstream/`.
