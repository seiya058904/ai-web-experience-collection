# MURMURATION — research and representation

Craig Reynolds' Boids, Ballerini et al. (2008), Attanasi et al. (2014), RANDOM INTERNATIONAL's *Swarm Study / I* and DRIFT's *Flylight* inform the reading around the work. The model is not an exact biological simulation: bounded neighbour search approximates “six or seven” neighbours, and the turning study is not reproduced frame for frame.
