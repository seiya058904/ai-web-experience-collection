# CODEX — The Anatomy of a Book

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose

An immersive digital craft work in which flat paper becomes a complete physical book through folding, ordering, sewing, enclosure and pressure. The visitor witnesses the birth of a book, rather than reading its contents.

## Users and success

The explicit audience is visitors to a flagship Web Design Showcase. Success is a tactile, continuous and reversible scroll experience with museum-publishing compositions that remain convincing while paused.

## Stack

Implementation choice for this request: vanilla JavaScript, Three.js, Lenis and Vite. No framework was specified. A single rendering authority and local, self-hosted assets fit the requested continuous scene.

## Capabilities and constraints

Ten scenes in order: The Volume, Sheet, Fold, Gather, Sew, Spine, Marble, Case, Press/Gild/Stamp, Codex. Required systems: physical sheets and folding, signatures, structural thread, spine, procedural marbling, cover wrapping, restrained gilding, physical typography. Lenis is the sole smooth-scroll authority. Desktop and mobile, bounded geometry and texture budgets, reduced motion, reversible assembly.

## Brand commitments

CODEX — The Anatomy of a Book. Museum publishing and dark luxury: ivory paper, black cloth, restrained gold. Editorial typography becomes embossed/debossed/foil-stamped material. No antique nostalgia, bookshop, reader interface, or generic photography template.

## Deliverable

ZIP only. Include source, one final build, selected AI imagery, models, textures, fonts and their licenses, README/build instructions and provenance. Local development/preview/build/QA only. Every deployment, including Sites, is prohibited. Exclude node_modules, caches, debug artifacts, temporary screenshots, rejected generations and duplicate builds.

## Evidence

The user's complete brief is authoritative. Craft references and selected generated concepts will be recorded in docs and art-direction. There are no commercial claims, customer metrics, or historical artifact claims to invent.

## Principles

- Assembly through order and pressure.
- One fold line becomes the signature center, page axis and hinge.
- Thread connects folded gatherings into a coherent text block.
- The marbling bath becomes a fixed material on a sheet.
- Every stable frame is a deliberate composition.
